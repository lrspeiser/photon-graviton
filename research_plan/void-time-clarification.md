# Clarified model: cumulative time stretching and companion transfer

## Agreed user direction

The proposed alternate-universe property of time progressively stretches light during travel through space, especially low-gravity voids. It is not ordinary gravitational time dilation and must not be repeatedly reinterpreted as a source/observer clock difference. The accumulated change remains after travel. This proposal does not assert that local galaxies have no redshift.

Every unit of photon energy lost through stretching enters the companion/graviton sector at conversion. That receiver is already specified. Companions may travel alongside the light or on their own paths; subsequent capture transfers their energy into deposits. No-loss companion travel and permanent deposits remain optional limiting cases.

## Current quantitative closure

The [cumulative-time report](../research_work/results/cumulative-time/report.md) tests a whole-signal closure: carrier cycles and event features stretch together by S=exp(integral alpha ds). This produces redshift 1+z=S and duration ratio S, while photon energy falls to 1/S and companions receive 1-1/S of the original energy. With fixed geometry and photon count, received power falls to 1/S^2.

The entire-signal closure is a candidate to derive and test, not an automatic consequence of photon-energy transfer. The reported propagation-clock example demonstrates the required frequency/timing mapping under a specified evolving law and matching endpoint standards. It is not an adoption of a varying-speed matter theory or proof that a fundamental companion action produces that profile.

## Next work and why it matters

Derive or test a time/companion interaction that generates the accumulated stretching, its rate and environmental dependence, with backreaction and momentum conservation. Derive companion propagation and capture using that same interaction. The destination of transferred energy is an agreed constraint, not a question to reopen.

Without the interaction we have predictions from a postulate, but not a first-principles cause. Without the capture and gravity response we cannot infer how much extra gravity the stored energy produces. Without a reliable timing estimator and the remaining observation tests, numerical consistency cannot establish astronomical agreement.

Earlier static-gravity comparisons remain historical diagnostics in Git history and other reports. They are not the user's proposed cause and are not the default next task.
