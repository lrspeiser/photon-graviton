Develop and test a conserved photon-energy-to-companion-to-deposit model against the [prediction checklist](prediction-checklist.md) and all 32 observational requirements. Work through the available calculations, expose missing laws and data, and retain permissible alternatives when a candidate encounters a problem. Preserve the original 20-task scope. The original time concept proposed that time/propagation behavior stretches light instead of space expanding, with that stretching causing photon-energy loss. It did **not** propose that local galaxies have no redshift. The user now permits a conversion-first mechanism without special time/void behavior as its cause. This is the active research priority, not evidence that timing observations have been explained or that all clock effects can be discarded.

## Current baseline for investigation

- Operational nonexpansion and fixed published galaxy distances, as required by the universe contract.
- Partial photon-energy transfer: a surviving photon becomes lower-frequency and companions receive accounted energy. Whole-photon removal remains a comparison, not a redshift explanation by itself.
- First forward model: dE/ds=-alpha E and z_conversion=exp(alpha R)-1, with constant alpha as the initial empirical benchmark. Do not assume it vanishes in local galaxies or derive it from expansion.
- No special void-clock gradient, changing atomic standards or changing photon speed is required by this benchmark. Timing effects must emerge from a completed interaction or remain an explicit unresolved requirement.
- Companions may be focused toward or captured near gravitational wells, but focusing, capture and stable deposition are distinct processes requiring separate predictions.
- Conservation includes fuel, photons, companions, deposits, recoil, drivers, heat and boundary flow. No-loss travel and permanent deposits are optional limiting cases.
- Companion identity, microscopic rate, stable storage and the joint motion/lensing response remain unproved. Do not make a strong-gravity multiplier or an independent dark-matter reservoir an unstated assumption.

## Work sequence and completion standard

1. Freeze the conversion-only observational assumptions and execute an exploratory redshift fit on recovered data.
2. Generate energy, photon-count, spectral-width, brightness and timing predictions using that same rate; distinguish exact mathematical tests from astronomical agreement.
3. Quantify capture and retention conditions, and explain what ordinary gravitational focusing can and cannot establish.
4. Map all 32 requirements to available formulas/results, missing laws/data, and next runnable tasks.
5. Build/test consistent microscopic candidates and their stored states; predict motion and lensing together from source energy.
6. Acquire a genuinely unexposed validation sample with a frozen protocol, then confront the complete theory with the full observation register.

Completion requires a consistent theory and declared observational tests, not simply an executable checklist or successful energy bookkeeping. This pass may establish narrower findings while the overall goal remains unfinished. Existing unfavorable results remain scoped to their assumptions. Ask about consequential new laws when needed; do not wait to perform already-authorized diagnostic comparisons.
