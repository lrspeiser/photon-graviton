# Shared Sun–Earth–Moon test of three reservoir branches

Protocol declared before calculation. User authorizes testing bound, independent and continually regenerated companion sources. This first calculation tests explicit minimal versions, not every possible version of each class. No new source energies, per-body efficiencies or graviton frequencies are selected to fit the outcomes.

## Common energy and gravity equations

**Hypothesized energy conversion; known quantum energy bookkeeping:**

    P_c,i = L_i*[1-exp(-alpha*D_i)],
    dot N_g,i = P_c,i/(h*nu_g)   (monochromatic example only).

nu_g remains unspecified, so power is determined but graviton count is not. The photon power lost equals companion power gained. This does not establish a microscopic production channel or its momentum/spin conservation.

**Conditional weak-field gravitational source identification:**

    M_c,i=E_c,i/c^2,
    Phi_c(r)=-G*M_c,i/r outside a spherical reservoir.

Stored-energy pressure and support must be included in a complete source; this is the same effective-mass bookkeeping used in the earlier tests. When testing a replacement of all ordinary gravity, set E_target=M_observed*c^2 and do not also add M_observed as ordinary mass. For an extra-gravity model instead, E_target must be the independently inferred excess, which is not supplied for these bodies here.

## The three hypotheses

| Branch | Reservoir equation | Minimal calculable choice | If ordinary source departs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: bound stored companions | dot E=eta*P_c, lossless storage | Constant source for common T, E=eta*T*P_c | Need the binding law to predict stripping or dispersal; the reservoir cannot simply be asserted to follow a removed source. |
| B: independent traveling concentration | dot E=P_c-E/t_escape | No extra self-binding, t_escape<=D/c; hence E<=P_c*D/c | Disperses on the crossing scale. An independently self-bound version needs a new binding/support law and is still open. |
| C: continuously regenerated reservoir | dot E=eta*P_c-E/tau | Steady E=eta*tau*P_c with common tau | Decays as exp(-t/tau) after feeding stops; released energy must have an accounted destination. |

The reservoir ODEs and exponential relaxation are known bookkeeping/mathematics; their identification with gravity sources is hypothetical. A and steady C have identical static scaling E=K*P_c with K=eta*T or eta*tau. Static mass comparisons cannot distinguish them; a time-dependent perturbation would be required. For a moving source use transport, e.g. partial_t u + div(u*v_transport)=q_capture-u/tau, with a dynamical rule for v_transport. Motion alone does not determine the energy-loss term or lifetime.

## Declared inputs and fit/predict rule

Use alpha=0.0002488993286382367/Mpc. As a fixed illustrative common geometry, set D_i=R_i: companions form over one additional body radius outside the emitting surface. This choice is not measured or claimed unique; results constrain this geometry. Take eta=1 as the maximal-capture normalization, while identifying that A/C only determine eta*T or eta*tau. The capture region extends to 2R; total reservoir mass comparison does not assume it is all inside the physical surface. Surface acceleration would require its radial profile.

Use nominal solar luminosity, and for Earth/Moon use the Sun's geometrically intercepted power as an approximate ceiling for reradiated-plus-reflected outward EM power. This is a common generous source proxy, not an independent measurement of their intrinsic luminosities. Do not count these reprocessed photons as new energy in the global solar ledger. Neglect small internal power, eclipses and secular changes. Solar and planetary mass/radius references are the [NASA Sun fact sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html) and the [planetary summary](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/). Rounded planetary inputs match our prior comparison.

Calibrate the single normalization K on the Sun's observed gravitational mass, then freeze it and predict Earth and Moon masses. This is an exposed consistency/transfer calculation, not a blind experiment or independent confirmation using dynamically inferred masses. Also report the full-conversion f=1 ceiling as a separate limit, without adopting it as the observed solar spectrum. No assumed cosmic age is imposed; K is an effective duration/efficiency product.

For B report the energy available before free escape, not an arbitrary fitted storage time. A genuinely independent self-bound concentration remains unclosed until confinement and an orbit calculation are supplied. A failed free-streaming limit is not a theorem against all self-bound fields.
