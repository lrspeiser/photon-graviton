# Research goals for collective gravitational memory

Set by the owner, 16 September 2026, against `main` at d55fa09. This is the controlling objective document for the path-memory line; individual protocols cite it rather than restating it.

**The central goal is to demonstrate that freely moving matter can build a shared gravitational field that helps support its motion — and then to determine whether that same mechanism predicts the measurements.**

That requires connecting successes that currently belong to different models: orbital support in a mature track (RUT-1 stage 1), stable memory-driven settling (PM-3 stage 1), collective cancellation of drag (RUT-1 stage 2C), and a competitive empirical acceleration law (PM-1, PM-2A). The collective result is the strongest immediate reason to continue: prescribed writers sharing a track retain almost all the inward support while sharply reducing drag. Formation from an empty field, and survival of a *disturbed* collective arrangement, remain untested.

**The standing rule of interpretation.** A result can establish a mechanism, constrain its parameters, or show that a particular version needs revision. It does not automatically become a verdict on every possible version of gravitational memory.

## The eight goals

| Goal | Question | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Build the field from nothing** | Can moving bodies create useful support without an externally maintained orbit? | A converged, freely evolving formation experiment. |
| **2. Make collective support robust** | Does drag cancellation survive imperfect spacing, different orbits and disturbances? | A map of the configurations that remain viable. |
| **3. Explain writing, retention and saturation** | What physical property of matter writes the field, and what sets its persistence? | A source-and-response law with a small set of universal parameters. |
| **4. Close the physical accounting** | Where do energy and momentum go, and how does the response propagate? | Coupled matter–field equations with explicit conservation and causality checks. |
| **5. Derive the right spatial force** | Can the mechanism generate the strength and radial dependence a galaxy needs? | Predictions from one shared source, not separate corrections per orbit. |
| **6. Test against measured dynamics** | Does the field describe radial *and* vertical motion with the same parameters? | Matched-source comparisons with uncertainties and held-out predictions. |
| **7. Predict something distinctive about memory** | What distinguishes this from a static law with the same rotation curve? | One quantitative, predeclared history-dependent prediction. |
| **8. Establish the domain** | Does the same physics behave sensibly for other motions, and for light? | A compatibility map and a stated range of applicability. |

Goals 1 and 2 take priority.

### 1 — A shared supporting field that forms without a prepainted track

The field must emerge from actual motion. The mature-track calculation deliberately held the field fixed; it showed a bounded perturbed orbit in that field, not successful formation.

Start from an empty memory field and a declared distribution of bodies, give them positions and velocities, and integrate motion and writing together. Isolated writer first as a diagnostic, then several physically distinct writers at the same total writing rate. Keep three controls: no memory, an already-built field, and the prescribed-orbit calculation.

After release: **no prescribed circular trajectory, no resetting velocities, no moving an old source ring to a body's new radius, and no adjusting the writing strength to reach a target orbit.**

Measure the field's inward contribution, each body's angular momentum, radial migration, oscillation amplitude, field-build time and any external work. **"Returns to the original radius" is not the definition of success** — a strengthening field may lead to a different orbit. The question is whether the system finds a bounded, supported configuration without unacceptable continuing migration. Report support, stability, settling and formation cost separately: a stable oscillating system can be informative without settling, and a circularizing one is not a success if it is spiralling in.

### 2 — Collective drag cancellation under realistic imperfection

Equally spaced, prescribed writers are a controlled best case. Build outward from it deliberately: perturb phases and initial velocities; then differing radii, unequal masses and writing rates, and small vertical displacements; then a finite-width annulus, and several overlapping annuli. Use multiple declared random realizations, not one arrangement that happens to work.

Two tests stay separate. **Numerical subdivision:** one physical writer becomes coincident weighted copies, and the field must be unchanged. **Physical redistribution:** writers occupy different positions, the field may change, and that change is the measurement.

Measure the net torque *and* the distribution of per-body torques — a small total can conceal some members migrating in and others out. Separate a body's own history from what the others wrote.

A conditional result is useful: "works for overlapping annuli but not isolated narrow rings" identifies the natural geometry rather than refuting the proposal.

### 3 — What writes the field, and what sets its lifetime

Does matter write the response because it exists, because it moves relative to something, because it accelerates, or because a collective state excites the field? These are different theories.

Baseline: `q_i = alpha * m_i`, one universal `alpha` — a proposed reference law, not an established one. Compare a few declared alternatives involving a shared field state, relative motion or velocity stress. Nonlinear sourcing must be defined on the physical continuum or collective state, never by assigning a nonlinear charge to arbitrary numerical particles. Discriminating controls: static versus circulating at the same density, co- versus counter-rotation, uniform translation versus internal motion. Any preferred frame must belong to a specified medium, not a coordinate choice.

The simplest model builds and forgets on one timescale. A worthwhile alternative is a rapidly excited state transferring into a more persistent stored state: `d(stored)/dt = writing − relaxation − transport`. The objective is not maximum retention at any cost, but a specified process that builds the response without demanding rewriting that destroys the orbit.

### 4 — Energy, momentum and causal propagation

The field must be a dynamical participant, not an immovable rail or an unpriced force generator. Track total energy across matter, field and interaction, reservoir, and outflow; likewise momentum and angular momentum. A fixed central potential or an externally held writer is acceptable as an early diagnostic **if labelled external**; a closed-system claim needs the reaction included. A variable that accumulates "missing energy" is a diagnostic, not an explanation, until its dynamics and coupling are specified.

Propagation is separate from retention. A causal completion must not influence a distant point before the signal can arrive. Test a localized source change, measure the arrival, and establish when the instantaneous approximation suffices.

### 5 — A galaxy-scale force profile, not per-orbit support

One source and one field should predict forces at many positions. The Gaussian family currently approaches **inverse-square** at fixed writing rate, retention and width; deriving the inverse-radius behaviour behind flat circular speeds is a separate claim. Build one extended source, hold its construction fixed, and probe across radii and heights. Never supply each probe with its own track depth. One fitted universal coefficient is permissible as an empirical parameter, but it must not then be described as an independently predicted acceleration scale.

### 6 — The matched observational comparison

PM-2A stages C and D: Newtonian, PM-1's local prescription, Completion I, Completion II and the simple-μ reference, all on matched sources. Stellar-tail continuation and removal stay declared sensitivities, never selected per force law. The Milky Way vertical force is evaluated alongside rotation, not afterwards. Universal parameters are fitted on the training galaxies only, with per-galaxy residuals, paired galaxy-level resampling, and both source-model and numerical uncertainty reported. **A difference smaller than solver or source uncertainty is unresolved.** A later dynamical memory model enters the same comparison with its own predicted fields; it does not inherit PM-1's score because its motivation resembles that law.

### 7 — A prediction that tests memory specifically

A successful rotation curve does not say whether the supporting field remembers. Extend the two-preparation experiment to the shared field: same initial source and field state, two nontrivial histories with driver work and momentum accounted, matched instantaneous matter positions and velocities at a common release. Then compare the fields and subsequent motion. Also useful: removing a subset of writers, disrupting and reassembling the source, and changing circulation at fixed density. The retained field must be *generated by the preparation*, never assigned at release. Compare against a memoryless static model calibrated to the same steady force. If no distinguishable signature exists in the accessible regime, the model may still be a useful static-response model and should be described as one.

### 8 — Domain, and how matter and light respond

Start with nearby, interpretable checks. Compare accelerations of different negligible-mass probes in the same field, distinguishing source-mass dependence from response-to-field dependence; composition-sensitive acceleration is constrained by MICROSCOPE and must be computed in that regime. Evaluate precession, timing and migration in a simple planetary or binary system, distinguishing measured ranges and angles from fitted ephemeris trajectories. For light, specify an actual propagation rule — an extra acceleration of massive bodies is not a lensing prediction without a derivation. Any transition or saturation must be part of one common law; a label such as "galaxy" or "Solar System" must never act as a hidden switch.

Redshift and background-radiation questions may stay open while the orbital mechanism is developed, and must not be counted as successes of it before a connection is derived.

## How the work is organized

| Workstream | Work now | Main dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Formation and robustness | RUT-1 stage 3; isolated and collective writers; phase and velocity perturbations | existing force and finite-age controls |
| Physical field model | writing law, storage, momentum exchange, causal propagation | analytic construction and small-system tests |
| Static observational benchmarks | PM-2A C/D | verified source models and field solvers |
| Distinctive memory tests | shared-field preparation experiments and prediction design | a specified memory evolution law |

RC-2b continues independently with its own source-energy accounting. Its best result must not be combined with a field-response result and called one demonstrated mechanism. For a single worker this means alternating independent tasks rather than waiting on a suite; for several, isolated worktrees and result directories, then a test of the actual merged tree.

**Exploration first, then frozen verification.** Exploratory scans map behaviour rather than aim for a green result. Once an interesting regime appears, freeze its equations, parameters, numerical requirements and metrics, then rerun on new initial conditions or data.

Every experiment distinguishes three things, and a result may pass one and fail the next:

- **Numerical correctness** — were the declared equations solved accurately?
- **Physical behaviour** — did those equations produce support, stability or a useful prediction?
- **Observational agreement** — did the prediction match data within the relevant uncertainties?

Reports characterize effects at several strengths, including more than a tiny correction. A nominal "10% support" setting is an experimental label; the actual support must be **measured from the evolved field**, never enforced dynamically.

**Negative results name what failed.** "This Gaussian, continuously renewed, isolated-writer model migrates inward under these conditions" — not "gravitational memory cannot work." Conversely, revisions must earn their success: changing retention, adding a carrier or introducing a vector field creates a new specified model whose costs and predictions must be retested. Calling the universe hypothetical does not remove the need for internally consistent equations.

## The next milestone

**A freely evolving, perturbed collective system that builds its field from zero, acquires measurable inward support, and retains bounded motion without continual external steering.**

That would establish a self-forming collective mechanism within a declared model — not the galaxy theory, but the largest gap between the current calculations and the original idea. After it, the decisive advance is using the same physical source and field laws to predict the force's spatial shape, its normalization, and at least one history-dependent observable.
