# Observational targets for a competitive companion theory

This priority decision supplements the six active research objectives; it does not mark any complete. The model may postulate a mechanism without explaining the ultimate origin of time. Empirical success requires shared, independently testable rules, not a separate adjustment for every observation. Agreement would support the hypothesis, not uniquely establish causation.

## First demonstrations

1. **Joint light propagation:** predict measured redshift, supernova spectral aging, light-curve duration and multiband brightness with one transport rule. Use distances whose inference does not assume the redshift law being tested. Preserve line shapes and account for wavelength-dependent constraints. Fit shared parameters on development data and score untouched objects. Current status: conditional calculators and spectral calibration work exist; a validated joint observational solution does not.
2. **Diverse galaxy motions:** predict rotation curves across bright, faint, gas-rich and dwarf galaxies from ordinary matter and a calculated companion source/capture history. Do not place deposits at the inferred missing-mass locations by construction. Current status: empirical force improvements do not establish photon-funded deposition.
3. **The same gravity bends light:** use the same stored source and gravitational response to predict stellar motions and resolved lensing. No separate lensing normalization. Current status: conditional lens pilots retain material discrepancies and structural uncertainties.
4. **Milky Way three-dimensional motions:** predict radial and vertical motions in the disk, bulge and above/below the bulge, with survey selection, populations and ordinary-matter uncertainty. Current status: data and partial comparisons exist; the full three-dimensional likelihood is incomplete.
5. **Colliding clusters:** calculate where deposits move when stars and hot gas separate, then predict lensing maps and retention. Current status: transport and receiver-selectivity requirements identified; no collision solution.
6. **An untouched distinguishing prediction:** specify in advance an environmental or path-dependent residual predicted by the selected mechanism. If the model predicts no such effect, do not invent it; choose another quantitative difference. Reserve observations and freeze parameters before scoring.

## Required for the broader cosmological claim

7. **Microwave background:** explain its spectrum, temperature and polarization patterns, correlations and lensing from a specified source and transport history. Do not import a Big Bang interpretation as an input, but retain the measurements.
8. **Large-scale structure:** predict galaxy angular/redshift clustering, including the observed preferred separation pattern, and its relation to the microwave sky. Do not import expansion-calibrated ruler lengths as independent facts.
9. **Element abundances and source populations:** provide a quantitatively consistent production/history model for hydrogen, helium, deuterium and lithium and observed stellar/galaxy populations. Universe age and size remain free; histories must still be finite or convergent where required and consistent across observables.

## Conditions across every target

- Energy and momentum transfer, reservoir stress/gravity, capture and transport must be explicit. An assumed energy balance alone is not a full physical derivation.
- Preserve successful local clock, orbital and light-propagation measurements; account for gravitational-wave/electromagnetic arrival comparisons including emission-delay uncertainty.
- Label equations as known mathematics, empirical fits, new postulates or conditional derivations. No unsupported novelty claims.
- Compare uncertainty-aware predictions, residual structure and parameter freedom. Prefer predictive distributions on untouched data; do not force exact equality to noisy measurements.
- Separate measured quantities from model-dependent distances, masses, ages and reconstructions. Excluding an interpretation does not exclude its underlying observations.
