# Goals for a postulate-based temporal-field theory

User-approved approach: begin with explicit physical postulates and derive testable consequences. Explaining why time, energy or gravity ultimately exists is not a prerequisite. A microscopic action is a possible later strengthening, not a mandatory first deliverable. Operational consistency, honest assumptions and predictions remain required.

Keep both selected branches: A, a shared temporal transport field for EM and gravitational waves; B, radiation energy feeding that field, with onward transport and capture in gravity wells. Do not collapse branch B into A's prescribed redshift rate and call the feedback derived. Preserve the existing four-stage goal in full.

## 1. Write the minimum rulebook

Why: without a fixed set of assumptions, changing a rule after every failure prevents a meaningful test.

Work: define the field state, energy reservoirs, ordinary matter, clocks, rods, EM waves and gravitational waves; state fixed spatial geometry, what interacts with what, and the allowed initial/boundary conditions. Distinguish traveling field energy from persistent field memory and deposited energy. Define which parameters are global and which environmental inputs are measured. Preserve energy transport out of voids rather than silently making permanent void deposits.

Deliverable: a numbered postulate/parameter register for each branch, labeling every formula as known mathematics, proposed postulate, conditional derivation or empirical calibration. Do not claim uniqueness without a literature assessment.

Finished when: every quantity in the evolution and measurement rules has a definition, units and provenance; open alternatives have separate branch IDs rather than contradictory simultaneous assumptions. Current status: partial branch specification exists.

## 2. Define what clocks and observers measure

Why: a wave that changes in coordinate units might show no measured redshift if the measuring clock changes by the same amount.

Work: specify emitted atomic frequencies, clock tick rates, rod lengths and measured local wave speeds. Ordinary-clock behavior may be postulated. Derive the source-to-observer comparison and identify whether a proposed completion changes spatial geometry, clock standards or local measured speed. State any departure from the user's original preferences openly; do not disguise it with coordinate terminology.

Deliverable: an observer/clock model with predictions in ordinary measurement units, including well-to-void-to-well propagation and the zero-field limit.

Finished when: observable redshift and event stretch survive the declared measuring procedure, or that branch is explicitly shown to cancel its own effect. A universal metric, microscopic origin of time or derivation of atomic physics from scratch is not required. Current status: unresolved; previous completions exhibit cancellation or changed local speed.

## 3. Derive redshift and the full arrival-time map together

Why: lowering photon energy, stretching an event sequence and delaying an entire journey are different predictions.

Work: derive frequency change, finite-event timing and absolute travel time from the same branch equations. Derive both messenger maps, with source-emission offsets explicit. Include frequency dependence across the EM spectrum and shared-GW response. Separate a postulated equality of transport laws from independent evidence for that equality.

Deliverable: a solver taking a distance, environmental history and emission signal to observed frequency, event shape and EM/GW arrival differences. Recover existing formulas only under their stated limits.

Finished when: each branch has explicit timing, frequency dependence and relative-messenger predictions; failed branches are recorded, not silently reinterpreted. Current status: conditional shared-ray identities and open-cell demonstrations exist, not a completed observation model.

## 4. Close energy transfer and feedback at the chosen model level

Why: the same energy cannot both remain in the temporal field and be counted again as a deposit; positive feedback can also produce unrealistic growth.

Work: define initiation/seed assumptions, finite transport speed, outward energy flux, capture and any memory relaxation. Track energy and momentum exchanges, including the cost of changing the field state at the operational level. Test no seed, weak radiation, bright radiation, long exposure, escape and capture. An explicitly phenomenological closure is acceptable if labeled; any omitted energy/momentum sector limits claims of full conservation.

Deliverable: conservation and stability tests over declared parameter ranges, with a physically interpretable budget and identified instability regimes.

Finished when: a retained branch has self-consistent bookkeeping and bounded, causal behavior in its declared domain, or its failure/required revision is demonstrated. No unexplained zero-cost field dynamics may be counted as a conservation proof. Current status: cell energy sums pass; full field cost, momentum, initiation and feedback stability remain open.

## 5. Define capture and the well's response

Why: knowing that a well receives energy does not specify where extra gravity appears or how strongly it bends light.

Work: postulate one common capture law based on a defined binding-depth quantity, distinguish capture from the field's effect on time, and specify how deposits affect forces and lensing. A deeper microscopic origin of gravity is not required. Derive spatial loading from transport rather than setting deposits equal to a desired halo. Test disk edges, bulge heights and clusters with ordinary-matter uncertainties. Keep the previously deferred total formation-energy supply explicitly unpassed; restoring that budget is required before claiming a complete gravity explanation.

Deliverable: a deposit-to-gravity rule and forward spatial predictions, with normalization assumptions and missing supply constraints visible.

Finished when: the branch computes acceleration and lensing from its deposits without region-by-region tuning, or a concrete discrepancy establishes the need to revise it. Current status: illustrative capture only for the newly selected branch; earlier gravity experiments remain conditional.

## 6. Calibrate distance-only versus environment-dependent redshift

Why: this determines whether the extra environmental rule predicts anything beyond an overall distance trend.

Work: use independently estimated distances, measured redshifts and documented tracer selection/sky coverage. Separate peculiar motions, distance errors and source effects from transfer redshift. Evaluate distance-only, void-dependent and radiation-feedback variants on the same calibration/validation design. Do not infer distances from the redshift relation being tested. Compare added parameters using predictive performance, not exact interpolation of noisy data.

Deliverable: actual-versus-predicted tables with uncertainties, parameter identifiability, environment sensitivity and selection limitations. If available data cannot distinguish variants, report a quantitative limit rather than a preferred cause.

Finished when: a defensible comparison is performed, or documented data limitations quantify which model differences remain unidentifiable. This does not turn an underpowered test into support. Current status: root rate fitted previously; independent-distance and selection preparation substantial, physical void comparison incomplete.

## 7. Test all linked observations with the same parameters

Why: matching redshift alone leaves many possible explanations; linked predictions distinguish them.

Work: test supernova timing, EM/GW timing with source lags, radio-to-optical/gamma frequency behavior, spectral-line widths, image sharpness, brightness/flux, laboratory and Solar-System clock/speed effects, and background-radiation behavior where the branch specifies a history. Include GW amplitudes/polarization if the branch claims a tensor-wave completion. Label predictions absent from the current model as missing, never passed.

Deliverable: observation-by-observation predicted/measured/uncertainty/status table with provenance and an exposure register. Known mathematics, fitted quantities and genuinely derived results stay distinct.

Finished when: every required joint prediction has been tested or explicitly identifies an incomplete candidate; only candidates meeting declared tests proceed to a confirmatory claim. Current status: many earlier candidate-specific audits exist; selected branches lack a complete joint pass.

## 8. Freeze a model and perform a genuinely withheld test

Why: a model adjusted after seeing the answers has not made an independent prediction.

Work: lock postulates, equations, parameter values or training procedure, nuisance treatment, selection, target list, metrics and pass/fail rules in a versioned manifest. Keep confirmatory observations inaccessible during final model development. Previously exposed galaxies, supernovae and GW170817 are development data, not fresh holdouts. Identify new compatible observations only with a clear exposure audit.

Deliverable: frozen manifest and reproducible withheld evaluation. If no complete candidate survives, publish that research outcome; it does not satisfy the active goal's requested surviving-model freeze/test.

Finished when: the frozen model predicts the withheld outcomes and the comparison is reported without retuning. Failure is a valid result; any revision requires a new confirmatory sample. Current status: not started for a complete model.

## 9. Write the paper at the strength the evidence supports

Why: a clear speculative model with checked consequences is a useful result without claiming that it explains the real universe.

Work: assemble postulates, conditional derivations, code/data provenance, known precedents, predictions, successes, failures and unresolved assumptions. Distinguish a theoretical proposal, a calibrated phenomenological model and an independently tested explanation. Keep GitHub main organized and synchronized with completed checkpoints.

Deliverable: updated paper and reproducibility package, clearly marked with its actual evidential status.

Finished when: each quantitative claim points to a calculation or source and no conceptual assumption is presented as a proven discovery. This writing goal can proceed alongside analysis; it does not replace the four-stage validation goal.

## Execution order and scope

Start with goals 1–4 for both branches, prioritizing the observer/clock rule before more catalog fitting. Develop capture (5) and selection-aware data work (6) alongside the resulting operational model. Use 7 to reject/revise branches; perform 8 only after a complete candidate is ready. Maintain 9 throughout.

The existing active stages map as follows: stage 1 to goals 2–4 and the messenger portion of 7; stage 2 to 6; stage 3 to 4–5 and 7; stage 4 to 8. Goals 1 and 9 organize and communicate the same work. No stage is marked complete by this planning document, and no microscopic explanation of why time exists has been added as a prerequisite.
