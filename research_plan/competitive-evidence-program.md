# Competitive evidence program

The user prioritizes a predictive effective theory before a microscopic account of its ultimate origin. Missing microphysics is not an automatic stop: explicit postulates may define the model. Conservation, internal consistency and quantitative cross-observation predictions remain required. This changes work order, not the nine-goal completion standard. Age and size remain unconstrained as stipulated; histories must still be explicit and energy-funded.

## Selected core demonstrations, in execution order

1. Joint light propagation: predict spectral redshift, transient event stretching and brightness/photon counts from one transport model. Fit only the declared calibration subset; predict the other quantities without separate adjustable stretch factors. Audit observer-frame photometry and source population variation so an assumed time-dilation correction is not mistaken for a measured result. Primary timing target: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05050 .
2. Galaxy diversity: predict resolved rotation curves for bright/dim spirals and dwarfs from ordinary matter and calculated companion supply, using common interaction/response parameters and explicit independently constrained object inputs. SPARC source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251 . Existing exposed galaxies are development data, not fresh tests.
3. Same-source lensing and stellar motion: predict image deflections/shear and motions using the same deposited field, with a declared metric response and no independently fitted lensing multiplier. Test raw/reduced observables with their dependencies, not a supplied dark halo.
4. Milky Way three-dimensional motion: compare plane-under-bulge, above/below bulge, and outer-disk populations with distance/covariance, ordinary matter, bar and selection uncertainty. Use velocity distributions, not a circular speed assigned to each bulge star. Existing APOGEE/Gaia/StarHorse data support this task; numerical convergence and likelihood remain prerequisites.
5. Cluster distribution and collisions: predict gas/galaxy/lensing spatial relations, capture and retention during motion; do not assume deposits occupy inferred dark-matter locations. A primary observational case is https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0608407 . Its dark-matter interpretation is not a model input; lens reconstruction assumptions require audit.
6. A discriminating environmental prediction: preregister the signed joint relation between independently estimated radiation/void history, redshift residuals and deposited gravity after controlling ordinary matter and selection. The sign and scale must follow the chosen mechanism before inspecting held-out outcomes. Do not invent a correlation or demand one every branch must have; if the model predicts no environmental signal, select another derived discriminator.

## Concurrent consistency tests

Local clocks, Solar-System/binary gravity, sharp spectra/images and electromagnetic-band agreement; gravitational-wave/electromagnetic delay with intrinsic emission lag; source fuel, heat and stability without imposed cosmic age/size. GW timing primary source: https://dcc.ligo.org/P1700308/public . These are concurrent checks, not postponed exceptions to a successful fit.

## Required for a full cosmological rival

Microwave background spectrum plus temperature/polarization correlations, large-scale clustering/acoustic features, element abundances and stellar/source histories. Compare measured patterns without importing expansion-based acoustic rulers or Big Bang chronology. Planck observational products: https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/planck . Success on the core demonstrations alone supports a serious candidate in those domains, not equivalence to a complete cosmological model.

## Evaluation and deliverables

Each demonstration requires a frozen model version, formula provenance (postulate/known relation/derived consequence/fit), input provenance, training/exposure inventory, forward predictions, uncertainties, residuals and explicit failures. Compare predictive likelihood or errors and interval coverage on the same held-out observables; report parameter flexibility and nuisance assumptions. Specify task-specific acceptable discrepancies before scoring, not one arbitrary percentage for all observations. Similar scores alone do not prove causal identity or establish statistical equivalence.

A complete microscopic derivation is a longer-term goal. The immediate decisive milestone is the joint propagation demonstration, accompanied by a frozen deposition-response specification that prevents independently tuning redshift and gravity. Existing passing numerical tests and empirical fits are foundations, not completed evidence for this program.
