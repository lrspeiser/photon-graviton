# Photon-generated gravitational waves: candidate clarification

2026-09-10. User steering asks whether the large photon-only delay comes from chosen constants and whether local conditions could accelerate photon-to-gravity conversion. This is a candidate/source clarification, not adoption of a demonstrated mechanism. The four-stage goal is unchanged.

## What was chosen and what was predicted

The 674,646-year result is conditional on (i) an inverse-affine evolving photon travel-time law, (ii) a spatially uniform positive rate, (iii) the rate calibrated from earlier exposed galaxy redshifts, (iv) unaffected gravitational-wave transport, (v) ordinary endpoint clocks and a nonnegative field age, and (vi) the stipulated 40.7-Mpc path. It is not observed and is not a necessary consequence of energy transfer.

The law dt/ds=1/c+alpha*(t-t_star) is a proposed postulate. The rate alpha=0.0002488993286382367 per Mpc is an empirical calibration, not a first-principles constant. The integrating-factor solution yielding the delay is known mathematics conditional on those choices. The field epoch was set to the most favorable nonnegative age for a lower bound; arbitrary negative ages would change the subluminal/no-advance assumptions. Changing the law or messenger coupling changes the tested candidate.

The [new rate sensitivity](../research_work/results/joint-propagation-audit/rate-sensitivity.json), calculated after the original result, spans the old exposed training bootstrap range c*alpha=72.417..76.887 km/s/Mpc. Delays remain approximately 654,680..695,231 years. That range is not a posterior over alternative physics. Within this same uniform law, the illustrative absolute intrinsic-emission bound of 10 seconds requires alpha at most about 1.383e-16 per Mpc. Its predicted transfer redshift at 40.7 Mpc is then only 5.63e-15 rather than 0.01018. Lowering the coefficient by roughly 1.8 trillion would remove the intended redshift as well as the delay. This is a conditional scale comparison, not a measured source-timing bound or a universal exclusion.

## A local conversion source is physically worth distinguishing

Standard electromagnetic stress can source spacetime curvature; suitable changing, anisotropic sources can radiate gravitational waves. A known concrete mechanism is graviton/photon mixing in an external magnetic field, the Gertsenshtein effect. It is theoretically described within Einstein-Maxwell physics and does not need cosmic expansion. It is not an established detection of photon-to-graviton conversion or a demonstration that it explains the measured merger signals. [Palessandro and Rothman, derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02072)

Three processes must be kept separate:

| Process | Surviving light | What still needs derivation |
|---|---|---|
| Whole-mode photon/graviton conversion in a stationary background | Converted fraction leaves the photon sector; surviving frequency is not automatically reduced | Mixing probability, background, polarization, coherence and propagation |
| Partial photon energy transfer into companions | Photons remain but lose energy: the project's desired redshift channel | Allowed interaction, outgoing spectrum, recoil/stress, event timing and clock response |
| Local coherent gravitational radiation driven by electromagnetic activity | Can add a source-generated gravitational signal | Tensor polarization, frequencies, phase coherence, amplitude, temporal evolution and photon-energy accounting |

**Known energy conservation, not a new interaction equation:** for a partial-transfer event, photon input energy equals surviving photon energy plus companion energy plus the net energy gained by the receiver/background. Momentum must balance too. This bookkeeping permits a hypothesis to be stated; it does not establish a nonzero transition amplitude or tell us which frequencies are emitted.

Stationary linear mode conversion preserves the driving temporal frequency. A converted optical or gamma carrier therefore does not automatically become an audio-frequency gravitational wave. Producing a low-frequency coherent gravitational signal from rapidly oscillating light requires an explicit collective, time-dependent or nonlinear source mechanism. The intensity envelope alone is not automatically the gravitational-wave phase.

If all relevant light and generated gravitational radiation then propagate at c, there need be no additional propagation lag from conversion. This is a distinct candidate from the tested evolving photon-only travel law. The source location/time distribution must be computed, not replaced with simultaneous emission by fiat. Distributed conversion along an unchanged common-speed ray can also deliver generated companions alongside the associated photons; it still does not by itself stretch the time separation of successive source events.

## Observational tests for this new source option

1. Define one environmental coupling that can be weak during long intergalactic propagation and strong under specified local conditions. Magnetic fields are a literature-based option, not an adopted value or proof of void behavior. Independently observed environments must set it; do not assign a different free rate to each object.
2. Derive the spectrum of the outgoing photons and gravitational radiation. Distinguish photon depletion from a shifted surviving spectrum before attempting a redshift fit.
3. Predict whether the generated radiation is coherent enough to give a detector-resolvable gravitational waveform, including both tensor polarizations, amplitude and phase history.
4. Compare a claimed origin of GW170817 with the recorded inspiral signal, not just the gamma-ray arrival time. Its evolving waveform is evidence supporting a binary inspiral; a replacement photon-powered interpretation must reproduce that evidence and the energy budget. [LIGO/Virgo observation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05832)
5. Separately test long-distance spectral/event stretching, absolute photometry and clocks. A successful local conversion source does not automatically solve cosmological redshift or companion capture/storage.

Our earlier chi*F^2 calculation concerns a scalar companion, not a tensor graviton. It found no source for an isolated ideal null wave in that setup, while colliding pulses did excite the scalar. That supports the need to specify conditions; it is not evidence that the tensor mechanism has already been derived. Preserve the [existing scalar result](../research_work/results/gauge-kinetic-transfer/report.md) and do not relabel it as gravity-wave production.
