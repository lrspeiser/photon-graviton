# Mechanism-first redshift: novelty and nonexpansion requirements

User clarification, 9 September 2026. Predictive redshift accuracy remains the goal, but empirical curve tuning cannot stand in for a new physical explanation. The exponential accumulation law is known mathematics and is not claimed as novel. No expansion rate or fitted expansion history is substituted for the microscopic rate.

## What must be derived

The candidate must specify an interaction, its physical degrees of freedom, energy and momentum exchange, matter clock standards, and environmental/source inputs. Solve these dynamics to obtain alpha before predicting target redshifts. A few universal couplings may be calibrated on training data, but no target-specific rate or residual-derived environment is allowed. Newness of the physical interaction must itself be checked against prior work before any originality claim.

The identity z = exp(integral alpha ds)-1 remains an output relation. It neither assumes expansion by itself nor demonstrates a new mechanism. The equality alpha=H/c under a suitable expanding-universe parametrization is not an identification adopted here. Path length, coordinates and reference clocks must be declared; arbitrary cosmological distances cannot all be equated with c dt.

## Concrete existing candidate to develop, not a finished discovery

Use fixed spatial geometry with a dynamical companion field phi and positive optical factor n=1+g(x) phi. For geometric-optics photons, H_gamma=c0 |p|/n. Take a positive canonical field energy with inertia K, wave speed v and potential V(phi). The illustrative shared Hamiltonian gives, for static g and smooth packet sampling:

K (partial_t^2 phi - v^2 Laplacian phi) + V'(phi) = sum_i [g(x) E_i/n_i] W_i(x).

Here each normalized W_i is the same sampling kernel used in n_i, and E_i=c0|p_i|/n_i. The right-hand side is obtained by minus the field derivative of photon energy, not an independently assigned source. If g or the environment is dynamical, its energy, forces and equations must be included. A fixed externally specified spatial g does not by itself establish total translational momentum conservation.

Along a ray:

alpha = partial_t n / c0 = g(x) partial_t phi / c0,

and dE_gamma/dt = -E_gamma partial_t n/n. Thus the rate is an output of the field/source dynamics rather than an arbitrary distance fit. This is conditional effective-model mathematics, not evidence that this field exists or is a graviton. The receiving field energy includes its background; energy already present must not be credited again to photons.

## Decisive next mechanism calculation

Investigate a spatially distributed, finite-energy radiation source in three fixed spatial dimensions, using the existing joined Hamiltonian as a declared candidate. The prior localized spherical source settles and loses its ability to generate new redshift; a one-dimensional rolling result does not resolve that failure. Determine whether a distributed source can sustain a positive, sufficiently uniform partial_t phi for the required duration without an imposed rolling field or hidden external driver. Freeze source geometry, boundary conditions, fuel and comparison cases before numerical fitting. Compare zero-source, localized-source and distributed-source evolution; test convergence, energy transfer, probe wavelength dependence and carrier/event timing.

Why: the universe-wide redshift trend requires a persistent rate, not just a static delay around individual sources. If this candidate settles, diverges, requires unavailable energy or depends strongly on each source's brightness, it cannot be promoted to the desired universal law. Do not prescribe phi(t) from the observed redshift curve and call the result a physical derivation.

Before claiming astronomical success, calibrate field units and source histories independently, account for atomic clocks, and predict galaxy-specific line integrals using independently available inputs. If a different interaction is needed, state its new postulates and derive the consequences before selection. No arbitrary new distance function is sufficient.

## Role of observational work

Continue the frozen external catalog audit and independent-validation work. Those tests constrain candidate mechanisms and determine failure, rather than manufacture novelty. The constant and smooth distance-rate comparisons remain empirical controls. Fresh-data quarantine, nuisance calibration, spectrum, timing, brightness, conservation, and eventual deposited gravity/lensing remain required. The current goal is not complete.
