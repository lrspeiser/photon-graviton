## Evidence policy

`primary_full_text_equations` means the relevant paper text and formulas were inspected. `primary_pdf_selected_pages` is narrower: the AQUAL first pages were rendered and inspected, with section/equation locations recorded. `primary_abstract` and `primary_publisher_abstract` do not imply a full equivalence audit. Their summaries are restricted to what the abstract establishes. Publication metadata can be supplemented by an author's institutional repository.

One previously cited author review (Favata) is retained explicitly as orientation, not as the original discovery. NIST DLMF is an official mathematical reference. The 34 inspected records are therefore not all original experimental papers, and inspection is not uniformly full-text.

Exact equation identifications rely on inspected primary formulas and independent algebra. Statements about complete physical equivalence require more than matching formulas and have not been inferred from abstracts.

## Access limits and follow-up leads

The Oldroyd 1950 publisher text, Stillinger 1976 primary article and Toomre 1964 primary PDF were not successfully retrieved. The first two publisher routes returned access errors; the ADS Toomre PDF timed out. Their bibliographic leads are kept separately, not counted as inspected equation-level evidence. Ashby-Pryer, Hohl and Chavanis-Sire provide inspected primary sources for the limited related context used in the report.

The Fort PMC page later returned a browser challenge. Its record is consequently labeled abstract-level in this audit rather than claiming that the entire paper was freshly read. Some arXiv HTML routes were unavailable and were replaced by the primary abstract, with that limitation retained.

No unpublished/private work, subscription-only full corpus, exhaustive citation graph, non-English literature sweep or patent database search was completed. No author was contacted. This is a bounded, substantive prior-art review, not proof that an equivalent complete model is absent.

## What a negative search means

“Not established to be previously published in this audit” is not “unique.” A renamed variable, different sign convention or eliminated auxiliary field can conceal an exact mapping. Conversely, a shared static limit or qualitative effect does not equate full dynamics, preparation, energy supply or observables.

This audit does not alter the scientific archive, including stage 8's failed numerical verification and unresolved quantitative comparison. Existing claims in historical reports are not silently rewritten; the dated provenance register supplies the corrective interpretation for future writing.
