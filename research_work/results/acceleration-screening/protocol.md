# Shared-action acceleration-screening candidate

Written before calculation. The user authorizes modified gravity. Treat standard-gravity failures as conditional comparisons, not a requirement to adopt expansion. This optional nonrelativistic candidate uses a preferred reference frame, fixed space and a scalar field; it does not yet implement physical matter clocks or constant local light speed.

Proposed here as a trial coupling; originality unverified: give the scalar a positive restoring mass squared m0^2 f(Y), where Y=|grad Phi|^2/a0^2 and f(Y)=Y/(1+Y). Thus the restoring force vanishes as acceleration goes to zero and tends to a finite value at large acceleration. The shape is stipulated, not fitted or derived. It also activates at force-cancellation points, a known limitation to retain.

Use the established Newtonian potential Lagrangian plus scalar kinetic/gradient terms and this one shared interaction. Vary with respect to Phi and phi, retaining the gravity reaction term; do not prescribe the environmental switch externally while dropping its force. The photon interaction is not varied in this subproblem; it would contribute to the scalar source in a joined model. No galaxy data are fitted.

Check symbolically the modified gravity coefficient and its longitudinal/transverse ellipticity eigenvalues at fixed scalar field. Examine dimensionless interaction amplitudes B=0,1,3,4,5,10. Record whether the spherical local force is enhanced or reduced, and whether the operator remains elliptic for all Y>=0. An unfavorable result must remain reported; do not flip an action sign merely to match the desired force.

Provenance: variational modified-Poisson structures and environmental scalar screening have extensive prior literature. This specific trial combination is proposed/tested here, not certified novel. It is a consistency diagnostic, not a complete gravitational or redshift theory.
