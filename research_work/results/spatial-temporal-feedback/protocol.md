# Spatial transport test of the radiation-fed clock field

Replace prescribed n(x,t) with the selected response n_t=kappa*T. Use a one-dimensional open domain [0,10], c0=kappa=1, positive-speed flux v=1/n for EM energy G, GW energy H and receiving energy T. Equations: G_t+(vG)_x=-(T/n)G; H_t+(vH)_x=-(T/n)H; T_t+(vT)_x=(T/n)(G+H)-Gamma*T; deposit_t=Gamma*T. This is a postulated common-speed receiving-sector transport, not a tensor-graviton identification. Include all boundary inflow/outflow in the reference-energy ledger.

Initialize n=1, G=0.2,H=0.02 and T=0.01*exp(-((x-2)/0.4)^2). Keep constant EM/GW inflow and zero receiving-field inflow. Compare Gamma=0 with Gamma=exp(-((x-8)/0.5)^2), plus a zero-seed control. The capture profile is an imposed diagnostic well, not a derived galactic potential. No observations or fitted alpha enter the demonstration.

Use positive upwind finite-volume fluxes and compare 80 and 160 cells. Track total energy including cumulative fluxes, minimum energy, max n, deposited/exported energy. Trace probes emitted at t=1 on paths [0,10] and [2,9], using the evolved field, its time derivative, and d tau=dt/n clocks. Retain endpoint factors; do not discard blueshift. Same messenger maps are imposed by the shared Hamiltonian. Verify the clock/event identity by neighboring-ray proper intervals and require absolute fine/coarse measured-redshift difference below 0.003 as an initial numerical gate, not a percent-accuracy certification.

The energy densities here retain the previous reference-time convention. Local measured photon energy is proportional to n*omega, not omega; reference-energy conservation alone is not a global proper-energy conservation proof. Field memory, gravity work and momentum still need a complete operational ledger. This calculation tests whether the existing response and transport can generate an observable spatial pattern, not whether a complete source theory exists.

Numerical amendments after failures: the initial neighboring-ray derivative check failed at loose ray tolerances; tighter integration and an emission half-interval of 0.001 resolved it. First-order upwind failed the declared refinement gate on the interior-start probe even after 320 and 640 cells; its results remain in first-order-results.json and can be reproduced with --first-order. The default transport was then changed to known minmod MUSCL reconstruction with face-centered speed and rerun at 80,160,320,640 cells. These changes address discretization, not the physical response, capture profile or seed. Failed coarse results remain archived, and the original 0.003 absolute-redshift gate is unchanged.
