# Working universe contract — draft

Fixed by the user: explore the same astronomical observations in a fictional universe; seek photons supplying a gravitating deposited sector; conserve energy; pursue the nonexpanding roadmap; keep alternative physical laws open; ask before choosing a consequential change of assumptions.

Do not fix prematurely: ordinary versus new companion identity, secondary energy loss, permanent versus finite retention, the universe's age/history, microscopic coupling, gravitational response per supplied energy and the receiving sector. Ordinary relativistic equations are comparison branches, not a reason to dismiss a fictional alternative without explaining its requirements.

No-loss companions with permanent deposits are an optional branch. Shared-loss ledgers with an explicit receiving sector also conserve energy. The two are distinct model specifications and must not share contradictory flux formulas.

Operational nonexpansion is to be checked with the chosen material rods and clocks. Existing redshift/clock obstructions stay in the register as conditional constraints that identify what a revised law must change. A formal definition of the final matter frame is still open.

Current data are all exploratory: 149 SPARC galaxies and the 26-object DustPedia overlap with their archived selections; cluster inputs contain hydrostatic and distance assumptions. No fresh holdout is claimed. Any added sources, changed distances, luminosity histories or matter laws must carry provenance and trigger a new forward calculation.

Acceptance thresholds for future observational tests remain to be defined before candidate selection. The baseline regression tolerance and dimensionless energy-test tolerance are not observational acceptance criteria.

Decision pending: which energy-supply/gravity-response direction to prioritize after the nearby no-loss sensitivity result. Work common to all alternatives can proceed while the user considers it.
