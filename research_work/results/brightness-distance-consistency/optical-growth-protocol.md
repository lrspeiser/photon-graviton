# Photon supply under a homogeneous temporal interpretation

No refitting. Test an additional interpretation of the existing optical-source requirement: a spatially homogeneous bath, D=c*(t0-t), no expansion, no net transport divergence, no non-photon injection, and conversion rate alpha*c at every photon frequency. This interpretation is optional and must not be presented as a consequence of the null-cone inversion alone.

Then z=exp[alpha*c*(t0-t)]-1 and du_c/dt=-alpha*c*(1+z)*du_required/dz. The homogeneous lossless companion equation is du_c/dt=alpha*c*u_gamma-Q_capture with Q_capture>=0. Necessary photon supply:

    u_gamma >= -(1+z)*du_required/dz.

Compute the observer derivative analytically from the fitted H series. Compare the lower bound with the measured microwave blackbody energy density and a broad historical UV-to-mm background intensity range. The latter is a scale benchmark, not a rigorous modern all-frequency upper limit. Include the microwave photons even though the original motivation was starlight. No assumption about their origin is necessary to use their observed temperature. Do not mistake a monochromatic optical intensity for integrated background power.

This tests the restricted homogeneous temporal completion only. Spatially varying incoming companions, another source, a different relation between lookback time and D, altered gravitational coupling or a different source allocation require their own equations and predictions. No cosmic age or size is fixed. A pre-existing large bath does not by itself supply its required present derivative.
