# Observer-regular metric-optics branch

Before fitting: test the additional interpretation that photons are metric null geodesics, D is accumulated local path length, observer energy normalizes the affine parameter, and S=exp(alpha D). Then dD/dlambda=S and lambda=[1-exp(-alpha D)]/alpha=f/alpha. With reciprocity D_A=D_G/S and a regular finite optical tidal field, the observer Jacobi distance has D_A=lambda+O(lambda^3). These are conditional branch assumptions, not facts established for direct non-geodesic photon conversion.

For D_G^2/D^2=B(f), this condition requires B(0)=1 and B'(0)=1. The previous B=1+eta f violates it when eta differs from one under these assumptions. Test the bounded postulate B=1+f/(1+q f), q in [0,100]. Its slope is fixed by regularity, while q sets saturation; do not fit that slope independently. Keep alpha,b=1, photon number, calibration, data and covariance unchanged. Fit q only on the 466 training rows and predict 494 farther rows. Include the no-area-correction benchmark separately; q=0 is not that benchmark. Report the conditional nature of the regularity requirement, all scores and any bound.

This enforces only a local necessary optical condition. It does not construct a global metric, derive field stress-energy, preserve arbitrary images by proof, or close companion production. All six goals remain open.
