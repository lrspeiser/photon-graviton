# Momentum of a newly captured cohort

Before computation: use existing p6,C1,R100 profiles at512 and1024 shells for kappa0=.1,10,1000. Turn incident supply back on only to evaluate the instantaneous source at that snapshot. Do not evolve or refit density. Conditionally assume photon-like companion momentum E/c, scalar absorption, and local merger of the captured directions into one cold massive cohort with no external impulse or outgoing energy. This is one explicit candidate formation rule, not an asserted property of gravitons or the only capture channel.

Compute local energy source q and net radial flux F. Obtain flux from spherical steady continuity and verify it against independent pointwise angular ray integrals at r=.1,.3,1,3,10 using256 and512 angle nodes. Compare beta=|F|/J, generated rest-mass fraction sqrt(1-beta^2), and mass-equivalent energy fractions initially below weak-field escape speed for c/v0=300,1000,3000. These speed scales are sensitivity assumptions, not observations or a fitted physical conversion. Require absolute beta differences below0.005 against the independent angular calculation and below0.005 across radial resolutions; require bound-source energy fractions within one percentage point between resolutions. Require 0<=beta<1 and exact local mass/kinetic plus momentum reconstruction to floating-point tolerance. Keep all failures. No observed/withheld data, circularizing torque, binding recipient or radiative cooling channel is added.
