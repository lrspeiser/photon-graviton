# Forward gravity of the component-attached source

**The density mismatch overstates the corresponding typical force mismatch.** Holding the inner component-density fit fixed, the median inner extra-force vector difference is 10.64%, with maximum 32.14%, relative to the existing empirical extra-force target. The prior 42.47% result was a worst source-density error and must not be interpreted as a stellar-motion error.

## Construction and provenance

We retain exactly the seven coefficients from the finer inner source-density fit. Only the bar, thick stellar disk and one gas disk have nonzero coefficients in that particular optimum. No coefficient is fitted to acceleration, stellar velocity or lensing here. Known linearity of Newtonian Poisson gravity gives a_extra=sum eta_j a_b,j. The storage interpretation is a project postulate, not a newly derived force law.

The nonaxisymmetric bar uses the existing full bar harmonic field. The weighted disks are solved using the same Legendre/Poisson method as the ordinary-matter field, with radial grids extending from 1e-5 to 500 kpc. Two numerical levels use 1536/3072 radial nodes, Legendre degrees through 128/256, and 512/1024 angular nodes. The finite numerical domain is not a universe-size constraint. The empirical target field and ordinary matter are unchanged.

## Interpretation and limits

Gravity depends on the source throughout space, so pointwise density differences can partly average out. The candidate is closer in force than in source density, but still has spatially varying residuals and cannot be declared successful from this comparison. The target remains empirical and unvalidated against the full observation set. No actual star, orbital-distribution likelihood or lens image is predicted here. These vector errors are not circular-speed or individual-star speed errors.

The result motivates forwarding this fixed candidate to measured motions, including vertical components and source/selection uncertainties, before rejecting it solely on source-density mismatch. A separate force fit, if later attempted, must be distinguished from this fixed-coefficient prediction. Capture cross sections, photon energy supply, attachment consistency and receiver motion remain unspecified, and adding component coefficients does not establish a microscopic cause. All six research objectives remain open.
