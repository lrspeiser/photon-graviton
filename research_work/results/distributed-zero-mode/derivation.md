# A finite radiation bath can excite a rolling homogeneous field

This derives one conditional consequence of the existing scalar-photon Hamiltonian, not a new fundamental law or a novel exponential redshift formula. Spatial geometry is fixed. A finite periodic three-dimensional volume has an exactly homogeneous sector; an isotropic photon ensemble has zero total vector momentum. The homogeneous field has no spatial-gradient energy. This special sector exists in three dimensions but does not test generic three-dimensional stability or a realistic cosmic boundary condition.

Let n=1+g phi with constant g, positive inertia K and zero field potential. The initial field and field velocity vanish. The initial photon energy density is U0. Homogeneous photon canonical momenta remain constant, so the radiation energy density at later times is U_gamma=U0/n. There is no new photon injection. The reduced Hamiltonian density is

H = K phi_dot^2/2 + U0/(1+g phi).

Hamilton's equation, rather than a prescribed redshift curve, gives

K phi_ddot = g U0/n^2,

n_ddot = A/n^2,  A=g^2 U0/K.

Multiplying by n_dot and imposing n(0)=1, n_dot(0)=0 gives

n_dot^2 = 2A(1-1/n),

K n_dot^2/(2g^2) + U0/n = U0.

The second expression is the exact energy ledger: growth of field kinetic energy equals photon energy loss. There is no continuing fuel supply and no initial rolling-field energy. The initial radiation supply is an assumption requiring its own source history before cosmological use. With A>0, integration yields

t = [sqrt(n(n-1)) + asinh(sqrt(n-1))]/sqrt(2A).

Thus n grows without bound, while n_dot tends to sqrt(2A). Field energy tends to U0; radiation energy tends to zero. Persistent field motion is inertial, not perpetual creation of energy. The zero potential is essential: a restoring potential changes this conclusion and must be investigated, not silently omitted from a completed model. Neither gravity nor a self-consistent fixed cosmic geometry is derived here.

The effective ray rate is

alpha(t) = n_dot/c0 -> (|g|/c0) sqrt(2 U0/K).

This is an interaction/source-dependent rate within the candidate. It is not yet a prediction in Mpc units: g, K, U0 and the matter clock standard have not been independently determined. Choosing g/sqrt(K) merely to reproduce the fitted alpha would be calibration, not first-principles confirmation. In this model doubling the initial radiation density increases the asymptotic rate by sqrt(2), a conditional source-dependence prediction to test. No expansion history or Hubble constant is used in the derivation.

## Light and event timing

For negligible-energy probes, dt/ds=n/c0 and d ln omega/ds=-n_dot/c0. Integrating gives omega_o/omega_e=n_e/n_o. Differentiating the fixed-path travel integral D=integral(te,to) c0/n(t) dt gives dt_o/dt_e=n_o/n_e. Therefore adjacent signal arrivals and carrier periods share the reference-coordinate stretch. Finite events can be distorted if that ratio changes during emission.

Spatial wavelength does not grow in this homogeneous sector: canonical wave number is constant while optical speed falls. Thus this calculation establishes a frequency/period ratio relative to the declared reference standard, not the user's full observed wavelength/atomic-clock interpretation. Source and detector experience different n values at different times. Their atomic transition frequencies must be calculated from the same matter coupling before identifying this ratio with observed spectral redshift. Earlier matched-wave clock failures remain relevant. No matter clocks are silently exempted.

## What geometry changes

The earlier localized spherical problem allowed disturbances to spread into an unbounded surrounding region and approached a static field. The periodic homogeneous sector has a zero spatial mode and no field gradients; its photon distribution is everywhere uniform from the initial slice. This is not evidence that a collection of isolated stars automatically yields the same solution. The different boundaries and initial distributions are material assumptions, not numerical details.

Check whether any approximate rolling regime survives over physical travel distances with finite source energy, and whether clocks and brightness remain compatible. No deposited companion halo, massless spin-2 identity or gravity enhancement follows from this result.
