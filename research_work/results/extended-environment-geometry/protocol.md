# Extended independent-distance geometry protocol

2026-09-10, specified before execution. The previous turn made progress by archiving a nearby feature-only catalog and measuring its inadequate radial coverage. Extend the geometry using the already archived CF4 individual-galaxy table and its byte-column metadata. Do not read velocity, combined-distance, peculiar-velocity or inferred-density columns into the calculation. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11238 . Method-specific moduli are registered to a common scale; they are not independent calibrations or a purely geometric catalog.

Record counts and distance/error distributions for all eight individual methods. The main coordinate prototype uses one measurement per galaxy with preference maser, TRGB, Cepheid, SBF; a missing or nonpositive quoted error disqualifies that measurement. Other methods remain available for a later source audit, not declared intrinsically invalid. Include every selected distance (no outcome-dependent cutoff). Use target IDs only to remove the 164 exact target galaxies from the tracer set; do not use the catalog's group-derived distances. Other members and calibration errors may remain correlated.

Use individual sky directions and D=10^((mu-25)/5) Mpc, a known distance-modulus conversion under stipulated published calibrations. Report asymmetric distances from mu +/- sigma and the small-error radial scale (ln10/5)*D*sigma. Count observational tracers in radial shells 0..11..25..50..100..200..infinity Mpc; density per survey volume is only sampling density, not physical matter density or a completeness correction.

Compute exact ray/sphere union geometry along all 164 already exposed target paths at radii 1,3,5 Mpc. As in the prior audit, the complement means no selected catalog neighbor, not a measured void. Keep all radii. Independently sample 64 realizations of Gaussian distance-modulus errors with seed 2026091004 for every tracer and target; directions fixed. This is an illustrative diagonal-error sensitivity, not the full correlated distance posterior. Report each target's central feature and 16th/84th sampled percentiles. Also compute one common +0.1-mag shift of all moduli, a declared calibration scenario, not a measured offset.

For each radius report the normalized two-column [D,V] design singular-value ratio, and the norm of V after projection onto D divided by norm(V). These are known linear-algebra identifiability diagnostics with equal row weights, not a likelihood or a void coefficient estimate. Compute them in every sampled realization. No new redshift outcomes, coefficient fits or claims of a fresh holdout.

Controls: compare analytic ray unions to independent midpoint integration on every twentieth target (20,000 points, absolute tolerance 0.02 Mpc); enforce 0<=V<=D; verify distances reconstructed from output moduli; verify all target IDs excluded; show null feature V=D gives a zero residual and rank deficiency. Preserve source hashes and extracted method table. If uncertain or incomplete coverage remains, quantify it and continue physical model predictions rather than fitting an arbitrary field to explain residuals.

Post-execution diagnostic amendment: total V uncertainty includes target-distance uncertainty, which also changes D. To avoid interpreting their common change as purely environmental uncertainty, additionally retain the central projection beta=(D dot V)/(D dot D) and calculate V_draw-beta*D_draw in each existing draw. Report its median 16th-to-84th percentile width with beta fixed. This uses no redshift outcome or fitted physical coefficient; it was added after viewing the original geometry results and is not a predeclared independent check.
