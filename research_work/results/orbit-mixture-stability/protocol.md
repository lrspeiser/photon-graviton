# Can common nonnegative orbit weights stabilize the verified population?

Use the eight common passing ordinary/candidate duration pairs, with their archived initial states and fields unchanged. Do not open stellar validation or test outcomes or fit gravitational parameters. This is a best-case numerical population-stability diagnostic, not an observed population fit.

For each orbit, form normalized time-occupation histograms in early/late halves of the first 244, 489 and 978 Myr. A single vector of nonnegative orbit weights summing to one must apply to all three durations. Minimize the largest total-variation distance between each weighted early/late pair by linear programming. Independently reconstruct the distances, check primal/dual feasibility and duality gap. Run both unconstrained weights and a diagnostic weight cap of 0.25, which requires at least four nonzero weights; the cap is not an astrophysical prior.

Test three declared resolutions: the inherited R/signed-z bins; inherited R/z/bar-angle bins; and those spatial bins jointly with cylindrical inertial velocity bins. Velocity edges in km/s are [-inf,-150,-100,-50,0,50,100,150,inf] for radial and vertical velocity and [-inf,0,100,150,200,250,300,inf] for rotation. Use the union of occupied bins to avoid an unnecessary dense phase-space grid. These choices define a diagnostic; no observational error threshold is implied. A 0.05 TV comparison is a stated numerical target (at most 5% moved between bins), not a measured tolerance or proof of equilibrium.

Repeat the histogram/optimization with every second saved sample. Compare equal weights and the optimized common weights. Preserve all solutions, weights and failures. A spatially stable solution may still fail in joint position/velocity; finite-bin/window stability does not prove a stationary distribution function. A failure only constrains this small eight-orbit library and these bins/windows, not the gravity theory. The eventual selected stellar likelihood, survey errors/selection, mass-model uncertainty, redshift/timing/clock/physical-stress/capture/lensing links and deferred energy supply remain open.

Supplementary sensitivity, declared after the primary minimax calculation: optimize the early/late halves of the 978-Myr run alone. This separates failure to agree with shorter approximations from failure of the longest available window. Preserve the primary result and thresholds, repeat the half-sampling check, and label this as a secondary diagnostic rather than a replacement success rule.
