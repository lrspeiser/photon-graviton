# CL-2 stage 1, amendment 2: gate G4 failed as declared on the narrowest galaxy width; the replacement, declared before it is run

Declared 19 September 2026, after the run of `cl2.py` as declared (archived unchanged as `cl2-results-as-declared.json`, numerical verification FAILED) and before the amended run. Nothing in `protocol-cl2.md` or in amendment 1 is edited.

**What failed.** G4 required the galaxy operators to change by less than 10⁻³ (scale-relative) when the source grid is doubled, on all 89 training galaxies. They change by up to **3.4×10⁻²** (UGC12506; eight galaxies exceed 10⁻³), all at the narrowest width, w = 0.1 kpc. Every other width converges: 6.0×10⁻⁴ at 0.158 kpc, 2.2×10⁻⁴ at 0.251 kpc and ≤ 6.7×10⁻⁵ from 0.398 kpc upward. The cluster half of the gate passes (5.2×10⁻⁵ against 10⁻⁴), and an exploratory check not declared as a gate finds the lens operators converged to 3×10⁻⁶ at every width.

**The cause**, found afterwards. The SPARC stellar profile is ln Σ interpolated linearly between the photometric samples, which are 0.1 to 2 kpc apart, so it has slope discontinuities at every sample; a 0.1-kpc footprint reads those kinks rather than a resolved field, exactly as a footprint narrower than the density shells did in the clusters. The declared run's galaxy fit leaned on that column (Λ = 6.5×10⁻⁵ at w = 0.1 kpc, w/ℓ = 1.5), so the galaxy readings of the declared run rest on an unresolved operator and are blocked under the protocol, as it says a failed gate must block what depends on it.

**The replacement, G4b.** Widths below **0.15 kpc** are excluded from the galaxy block, as widths below 10 kpc already are from the cluster block and for the same reason: their columns are set to zero there, and they remain in the family, constrained by the lenses only (whose light profiles are analytic and converged). G4b requires the *retained* galaxy columns to change by less than 10⁻³ under grid doubling on all 89 training galaxies, and the excluded column's change is reported beside it. The tolerance is unchanged; only the set of columns it applies to is, and the boundary is the measured resolution of the source, not a choice made on the fit.

**What this does and does not reach.** Every cluster reading of the declared run is unaffected (its columns never included the failing widths). The galaxy, lens and transfer readings are recomputed in the amended run and are reported beside the declared run's, which stay in their archive as they fell. If G4b fails, nothing further is declared in its place without the owner. Wherever the amended verification status is quoted, the declared gate's failure is quoted with it.
