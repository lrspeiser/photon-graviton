# CL-2 stage 2, amendment 2: the count of widths in the family
Declared 19 September 2026 before the archived run. The protocol writes "the 21 widths 0.158 kpc to
10 Mpc" in S2-2 and S2-5. The family is stage 1's 26-width grid without its 0.1-kpc column, which is
25 widths, five per decade from 0.158 kpc to 10 Mpc. Every computation in TF-1 and in this stage's
development uses all 25; the number 21 was a miscount in the text, repeated from TF-1's report and
protocol, and is corrected there by a note. No gate, threshold, run or family changes.
