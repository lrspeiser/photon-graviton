# Publication checkpoint

We can responsibly release an exploratory methods-and-results manuscript and the reproducible research archive. We cannot yet describe the photon-to-companion proposal as a validated theory explaining redshift and missing gravity. A journal's acceptance is a separate assessment; reproducible material is not a guarantee of acceptance.

## What we can present now

1. **The hypothesis and its testable requirements.** Describe a nonexpanding hypothetical universe with photon energy transfer, onward-moving companions and a deposited gravitational contribution. State explicitly which parts are postulates, familiar mathematics, empirical fits and conditional deductions. Do not claim the exponential redshift law, standard gravitational potential machinery or quantum identities as unique discoveries.
2. **Reproducible empirical gravity results, including disagreements.** We have a three-dimensional conservative extra-potential prototype and conditional rotation benchmarks. In the frozen Cepheid validation, 167 retained stars form nine scored bins: RMS falls from 67.74 km/s for the original ordinary-matter model to 15.36 km/s for the primary added field. However, the primary is low in all nine bins. This is useful transfer to reserved stars under documented shared assumptions, not proof of photon-generated gravity. [Validation evidence](https://github.com/lrspeiser/photon-graviton/blob/2983a90/research_work/results/cepheid-validation/report.md).
3. **Mechanism constraints and unsuccessful constructions.** The energy/momentum and quantum-receiver tests identify conditions under which particular maps conserve quantities, preserve or destroy frequency coherence, or consume receiving resources. Such results can be presented as conditional calculations and limitations, not a completed physical interaction. [Release audit](https://github.com/lrspeiser/photon-graviton/blob/2983a90/research_work/results/quantum-companion-release/report.md).
4. **The data and numerical methods.** Catalog inventories, provenance, selection rules, scripts, frozen tests and numerical failures are substantial reproducible material. The local APOGEE, Gaia subset, StarHorse and BRAVA catalogs are available; the joined sample has 140,407 entries. Having data does not itself complete the population likelihood.

The GitHub archive is already public. The existing academic manuscript is v0.5 with evidence frozen through commit 4f83fe5. It predates several important receiver and orbit results and needs an evidence and claims update before being presented as the current account. This checkpoint is not that manuscript revision or a journal submission.

## What still needs work, and why

| Requirement | Current position | Why it is necessary / consequence of leaving it open |
|---|---|---|
| A physical cause of photon frequency loss | Several stipulated transfer and time-field constructions exist; no completed local interaction derives the rate alpha. | A formula fitted to redshift tells us how much loss to assign, but not why it occurs or how to predict a different environment. |
| One explanation for spectrum, event timing and clocks | Particular models meet some conditions and fail others. Losing photon energy at fixed travel speed does not automatically stretch an entire event. | The same theory must explain the colors of the light and the observed pace of changing sources while retaining the specified local clocks. Separate adjustable rules would not be a unified explanation. |
| A predictive connection from lost light energy to extra gravity | The gravitational amplitude remains independently adjustable. Changing alpha can be compensated by changing that amplitude. | A good rotation fit cannot identify photons as the source of the extra gravity while that freedom remains. We need an independently fixed production, capture and gravitational-response rule. |
| Capture, retention and complete energy/momentum accounting | Restricted calculations balance their displayed sectors; no complete traveling-and-depositing mechanism is established. The total astronomical energy supply was deferred. | We must explain why companions leave voids, accumulate in suitable locations and remain there, including what absorbs their momentum and any supporting or receiving resources. Energy supply cannot be counted as passed simply because an exchange equation balances. |
| Reliable three-dimensional stellar predictions | Catalogs and a potential exist; a stable, selected orbit-population likelihood is unfinished. Longer sampling helps a targeted orbit, but numerical field representation changes late occupations. | Otherwise a numerical or sampling error can look like an extra gravitational effect, especially when comparing the plane, bulge and outer disk. |
| Joint observational performance and independent validation | Rotation improvements coexist with residual bias and unresolved vertical, lensing, timing and thermal-background tests. No comprehensive acceptable-fit result exists. | One rule must predict different kinds of observation with consistent parameters. After choices are frozen, final holdouts must be evaluated without tuning them away. Already exposed tests cannot be repeatedly called fresh validation. |

The amplitude issue is a demonstrated identifiability problem, not just a missing preference: the earlier calculation changed photon-loss rates by factors of 0.01 through 100, compensated the gravitational amplitude, and preserved all 3150 retained rotation predictions from 149 galaxies to floating precision. [Theory-level review](https://github.com/lrspeiser/photon-graviton/blob/2983a90/research_work/results/theory-priority-review/report.md).

## The latest result and what it changes

The just-completed [field-population test](../orbit-field-population/report.md) found that two representations differing by at most about 0.161% in added force along the sampled paths give meaningfully different late occupations of one targeted orbit. In one near-plane radial cell, the final interval's mean rotation differs by 3.32 km/s and rotational spread by 7.06 km/s. Those are numerical sensitivity measurements, not observed star discrepancies or confidence limits.

This does not overturn the earlier conditional Cepheid validation, which used a different fixed approximate pipeline. It means that the new long-orbit bulge/disk machinery cannot yet be promoted as certified merely because it conserves its Hamiltonian or passes a local force comparison.

## Work order

1. Refresh the manuscript and evidence map so that they report current successes, failures, formula provenance and holdout history honestly. Frame the release as an exploratory framework with quantitative constraints, not a discovery announcement.
2. Prioritize the two missing physical connections: a shared spectrum/timing/clock mechanism, and an independently predictive link between photon loss and deposited gravity. More orbital precision alone cannot supply either connection.
3. Develop population-level numerical convergence and the survey-selection/error-aware stellar likelihood to the accuracy needed for the actual motion predictions. Include a broader paired orbit set and ordinary-matter uncertainty.
4. Freeze the resulting model and analysis before opening remaining final tests. Publish adverse results and limitations as well as improvements; a strong case is an outcome to earn, not a result guaranteed by tuning.

The full goal remains active. The present supported claim is a documented exploratory research program with empirical improvements and identified obstacles, not a comprehensive match to our universe.
