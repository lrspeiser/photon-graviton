# Inward migration variants: declared before execution

Use the completed stream-capture normalized profiles without changing capture, one-third retention, or normalization. Test distant illumination and the nearest tested source shell (D/R=1.1) separately, without fitted source mixtures. These are conditional source inputs, not observed source populations. Each target retains its diagnostic R=5 Re.

Four phenomenological inward maps for x=r/R, b>=0, s=exp(-b): (1) all deposits contract to y=s*x; (2) fraction f contracts while the rest stays; (3) a support radius h stops contraction, y=x for x<=h and y=h+s*(x-h) otherwise; (4) retention-conditioned contraction y=exp[-b*(1-eta)]*x. b represents an unspecified migration rate times elapsed duration, not an adopted cosmic age. Maps (1),(3),(4) solve elementary first-order relaxation laws; their application to captured companions and their dimensionless scaling by R are proposed, not derived physics. A support radius specified in units of the diagnostic boundary is phenomenological.

Bounds: b in [0,ln(100)], f in [0,1], h in [0,0.5]. Fit each candidate with shared parameters across six galaxies separately for each geometry, population proxy and source branch. Also refit on five galaxies, freeze parameters and evaluate the sixth. Existing halo targets have already been inspected, so leave-one-out transfer is not a blind observation test. Minimize equally weighted mean squared cumulative-fraction error sampled uniformly in r/R. Report each target's maximum cumulative error and cohort RMS, including adverse outcomes and boundary hits. Retain all variants; do not expand bounds after results.

Conserve inventory by pushing the cumulative distribution through each monotonic map. No absolute supply, gravitational-work conservation or support stress is solved by this operation. This is a finite-time placement test, not a new rotation/lensing likelihood fit. Verify baseline at b=0, monotonicity, total retained inventory, inward CDF ordering and interpolation-grid stability. Do not call a close target fit a mechanism derivation.
