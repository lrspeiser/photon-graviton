# Local compact/extended exchange: declared before execution

Keep the one-third capture law and original finite-region supplied inventory. Split each captured shell between an extended population staying at r and a compact population moving to s*r. This reuses the previous contraction transport as a controlled comparison; s remains phenomenological, not a derived support radius. No target halo quantity enters the exchange probability. Use both source branches, geometries and population proxies.

Known two-state rate balance gives f=k_on/(k_on+k_off). Test three proposed ratios using the ORIGINAL companion-fit stellar Hernquist potential, B(r)=G M_star/(r+a_star), a_star=Re/1.8153:
1. Binding: k_on/k_off=B/v_ex^2.
2. Released binding: k_on/k_off=[B(s*r)-B(r)]/v_ex^2.
3. Occupancy penalty: k_on/k_off=B/{v_ex^2[1+rho_input/rho_star]}.

Thus stronger binding favors compact entry; the third prescription reduces it when companion density relative to stellar density is higher. The density ratio is a proposed crowding proxy, not a measured saturation capacity. All fields are evaluated on the initial captured state: this is a frozen-environment exchange closure, NOT a self-consistent equilibrium with updated self-gravity or occupancy. The overall rate cancels, so no relaxation time or cosmic age is inferred. Moving after exchange does not establish detailed balance at the destination.

Grid: s=0.03..1, 49 logarithmic values; v_ex=10..3000 km/s, 41 logarithmic values. Both parameters shared across six targets; refit on five and freeze for the omitted sixth. Fit equal-galaxy/radius mean squared cumulative profile differences, not new stellar/lensing data. Archive boundary flags, per-target compact fractions and errors. These targets have been inspected before; transfer is not blind discovery. No grid expansion after results.

Use 2048 equal-radius source cells and 401 output radii. Verify with 4096 cells; check nonnegative compact/extended masses, conservation, monotone CDFs, inward ordering and s=1 baseline. Compare the prior partial-migration transfer on the same metric. Normalized shapes do not establish an absolute source budget. Gravitational work and kinetic/support energy remain explicit missing channels, despite conserved mass-equivalent inventory. No new test of motion/lensing is warranted unless the profile benchmark improves robustly.
