# Receiving-capacity-limited settling: declared before execution

Keep the one-third law and capture parameters fixed. Use each prior stream shape as a source of packets, with total mass-energy fixed to the ORIGINAL deposited inventory inside the same diagnostic 5 Re region (never the NFW target mass). Test distant and near-1.1R inputs, both geometries and population proxies. Compare to normalized halo targets as before; this remains a placement test, not a successful absolute halo-mass fit.

Three capacities for the BOUND reservoir: constant mass-equivalent density C; capacity zeta times ordinary stellar density; and density C*g_b/g_ref, g_ref=1e-10 m/s^2. For the last two use a declared spherical Hernquist proxy with scale Re/1.8153 and the original companion-fit stellar mass, not the target NFW mass or potential. This is an approximate baryonic proxy, not the full previous stellar model or an independent mass observation. Geometry comparisons retain the original capture inputs.

For each packet initially at r, seek the innermost available shell between s*r and r, where s=exp(-b). Fill its remaining bound-energy capacity, then move outward through that interval. Any remaining packet cannot bind and is kept in an explicitly counted traveling reservoir. It is not deleted, renormalized into the bound halo, or declared nongravitating. Process inner source shells first; reverse ordering is a sensitivity check. This is a discrete finite-migration allocation rule, not a derived continuum drift solution. No outward settling or sources outside the diagnostic region are introduced.

Grid: b=0..ln(100), 33 values. log10(C/Msun kpc^-3)=3..12, 37 values; log10(zeta)=-3..3, 37 values. 160 radial cells; double to 320 for chosen shared and leave-one-out parameters. Fit two shared parameters per branch by mean squared cumulative error with equal galaxy/radius weighting; explicitly preserve the old no-migration baseline as a separate control. Report every branch, parameter-grid boundaries, blocked fractions, capacity and inventory checks, resolution and ordering effects. Refit five targets and evaluate the sixth; all targets already inspected, so not blind evidence. Do not expand grids after results.

Bound CDF is divided by supplied inventory, NOT its own bound total: failure to bind is penalized, including at R. Capacity controls bound states; traveling energy also gravitates and would need its own stress/transport calculation. No claim of full energy conservation including gravitational work, or of a new lensing fit, follows from conserving packet energy alone.
