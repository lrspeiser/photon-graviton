# Wave support and orbiting particles: declared comparison

Keep one-third capture and the original finite-region deposited inventory fixed. Use original companion-fit stellar masses with a spherical Hernquist proxy a_star=Re/1.8153. Do not use NFW target mass or potential in the support rules. Both geometry targets and population cases are retained. Targets are already inspected; leave-one-out is conditional transfer, not blind validation.

Wave branch: a nonrelativistic massive scalar analogue, not ordinary massless gravitons. Gaussian density rho=M/(pi^1.5 a^3) exp(-r^2/a^2). Minimize specific energy e=3 h_eff^2/(4a^2)-GM/(sqrt(2pi)a)+<Phi_star>+K M/[(2pi)^1.5 a^3], where h_eff=hbar/m and K>=0 is a repulsive P=K rho^2 coefficient. <Phi_star> is the Gaussian expectation of -G M_star/(r+a_star). Test K=0 and K>=0 separately. h_eff grid 10^1..10^5 kpc km/s, 41 values; K grid zero plus 10^-8..10^-1 (Msun^-1 kpc^3 (km/s)^2), 22 values. Minimize over a=1e-4..1e5 kpc. Gaussian is a variational restriction, not a solved Schrodinger-Poisson ground state. Check positive curvature against Gaussian expansion/contraction and parameter/radius boundaries; do not claim full perturbative or nonlinear stability.

Particle branch: randomly oriented circular orbits, with a conserved angular-momentum prescription j(q)=lambda sqrt[G r0(q)(M_star(<r0)+q M)]. Solve j^2/G=r[M_star(<r)+q M] for each cumulative mass shell q. lambda grid 0.05..1.5, 117 values. The source r0(q) comes from the fixed captured profile. lambda=1 reproduces it; lambda is a shared kinematic assumption, not angular momentum derived from arriving photons. Test distant and nearest-source profiles separately. Check positive epicyclic frequency in the resulting positive-density fixed potential (individual-orbit stability only); collective stability and formation dissipation remain open.

Compare enclosed mass / supplied M with normalized halo targets on uniform r/R grid (R=5 Re), without renormalizing material outside R back into it. Compare archived partial-migration predictions on the same grid. Fit shared parameters on all six and, separately, five with the sixth frozen for evaluation. Report all branches and grid bounds; no post-result range expansion. If a branch improves, retain absolute mass, outer material and support assumptions as prerequisites to any new lens/motion fit. No fixed cosmic age or new observational success is implied.

Verify stellar-potential quadrature, scalar-energy minima, particle force balance, input-profile recovery at lambda=1, normalization and radial numerical refinement. Full packet-to-bound-state conversion, scalar effective mass origin, angular momentum transfer, radiation of settling energy and source histories are not supplied by this equilibrium test.

Execution validation: some particle parameter values reverse radial mass-shell ordering. Reject these as invalid members of this ordered circular construction before scoring; retain invalid counts. Training fits exclude invalid configurations using their training targets only. If the chosen rule fails on an omitted target, it must be reported as a failed transfer, not silently retuned. No such failure occurred for the selected shared/omitted fits.
