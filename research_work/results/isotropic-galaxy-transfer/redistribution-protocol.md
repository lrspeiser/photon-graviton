# Redistribution of the exact-one-third deposit profile

Declared before fitting. Preserve q=1/3, X, opacity, capture constants, ordinary matter and the 89/29/31 galaxy split. This experiment moves the deposited profile; it does not refit the capture or energy normalization. Use the same equal-galaxy mean squared log speed objective on 89 training galaxies only. Comparison sets are already exposed and are not blind evidence.

Known conservative radial scaling: rho_s(r)=rho_0(r/s)/s^3, M_s(<r)=M_0(<r/s). Proposed companion interpretation: captured deposits migrate before settling. Mixture rho=(1-f)rho_0+f*rho_s conserves the inventory for 0<=f<=1. This is not a derived migration mechanism; gravitational work and support remain open.

Run the following bounded family, then stop rather than adding target-specific rescue terms:

1. Shared dilation s in [1/4,4], one fitted parameter, f=1.
2. Partial redistribution, f in [0,1], s in [1/4,4], two fitted parameters.
3. Retention-conditioned dilation s=exp[b*(1-eta)], b in [-ln4,ln4], one fitted parameter. eta remains exactly X^(1/3)/(1+X^(1/3)). This proposed extension ties redistribution to our existing retention probability; its exponential parametrization is known mathematics, not a first-principles result.

Fit several starts. Check integration refinement and reproduction of the unchanged baseline. Freeze each candidate and run all six lens systems and both luminosity proxies with the same prior inner stellar nuisance fit; no candidate selection based on lens results. Preserve prior outputs. Report boundaries, fractional and absolute rotation scores, per-galaxy wins, and lens/outer-motion scores. A training improvement alone does not count as joint success.

Interpretation: s>1 moves energy outward and reduces enclosed mass at fixed radius; it cannot automatically increase rotation. Total deposited inventory remains fixed, but total gravitational plus kinetic energy requires a future dynamical completion. No new age, size, luminosity, or per-target normalization is introduced. Coma's free shape fit cannot test the one-third normalization and is not counted as a new transfer success.

Post-fit boundary follow-up, declared before execution: the partial model selects s=1/4 at its lower bound and f=0.01572. Extend only that candidate to s in [1/16,1] with the same fixed one-third capture constants, preserving original files. Use denser radial tables spanning R/s, six optimizer starts and the same refinement test. Freeze and evaluate lenses regardless of outcome. If it remains boundary-limited or worsens lensing, do not keep narrowing the scale to manufacture a joint success. This is a post-result diagnostic, not a preplanned independent discovery.
