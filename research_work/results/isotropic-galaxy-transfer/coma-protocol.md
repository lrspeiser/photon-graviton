Declared before fitting. Use the six previously reconstructed Kubo Figure 2 tangential-shear points with plotted errors. Fit only bins 1-3; freeze for 4-6. Radii enter only as ratios to the first plotted radius, so no h-dependent physical distances are adopted. Bin centers approximate unknown source-weighted annular predictions. Keep negative observations. Missing covariance, source geometry and exact annular weighting limit this to descriptive shape transfer.

For the same opacity shape kappa proportional to (1+x^2)^-2, calculate deposited shape rho(x)=(1+x^2)^-2 <exp(-tau)> for fixed central chord optical depths T=0 (transparent approximation) and T=100 (strong-interception diagnostic chosen before fit). These are two declared limits, not fitting T or claiming the SPARC physical k0 has transferred. Project with known spherical lensing to F(y)=mean Sigma(<y)-Sigma(y). Fit positive amplitude B and radial scale a: gamma_t(R)=B F(R/a). Fit log10 a in [-2,2] in units of first plotted radius; profile positive B analytically on training bins. Use a fixed scan and bounded local minimization; do not select T by outer results.

Also fit a point-mass shape B/R^2 on the same inner bins as a reference. All models have zero cross shear. This tests whether a shape could dominate total shear, not an ordinary-plus-deposit cluster model: ordinary gas/stars have not been subtracted, and their spatial contribution is a material missing requirement. No mass, exposure or energy-supply interpretation of B is justified. The shear approximation neglects reduced-shear corrections. No model can be accepted as a solution from these six bins.
