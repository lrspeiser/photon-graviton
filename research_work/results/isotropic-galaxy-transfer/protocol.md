# All-direction interception on actual galaxies

Before fitting: an isotropic incident companion bath crosses a fixed spherical capture coefficient kappa(r)=k0/[1+(r/a)^2]^2, a=s R_disk. This declining opacity is a candidate postulate tied to measured stellar scale length, not a derived interaction or deposited-density assignment. It is integrable without a physical cutoff. Calculate straight-ray optical depth from infinity, angular attenuation J=<exp(-tau)>, then rho_d=C J/[1+(r/a)^2]^2 with C=(k0/c) integral u_external dt. Companions lose energy only by capture. No fixed universe age/size is used; fitted exposure is not computed stellar supply. Retention/support, focusing, nonspherical structure and opacity feedback remain absent.

Fit three common log10 parameters C in [-2,12] Msun/kpc^3, k0 in [-6,2] kpc^-1, and s in [-1,2] on 89 historical SPARC training galaxies. Starts (7,-2,0),(7,-1,.5),(6,-4,1). Also fit a transparent control J=1 with C,s free, matching starts but excluding k0. It is the undepleted approximation, not a literal finite deposit from zero opacity. Keep original ordinary gas and stellar contributions, distances and 29/31 evaluation groups fixed. Equal-galaxy squared log10 speed objective. Freeze parameters for all evaluation galaxies; no individual mass/exposure fit.

Compute radial integrals on 256 intervals, angular quadrature 32 nodes; evaluate fitted predictions at 512 intervals/64 nodes. Require group RMS drift below 0.1 km/s, otherwise retain failure and refine fit. This verifies the actual prediction calculation, not data resolution. Report actual/predicted curves, parameters, boundaries, all starts and control comparison. Reused partitions mean exploratory transfer rather than blind evidence. All six goals remain open.
