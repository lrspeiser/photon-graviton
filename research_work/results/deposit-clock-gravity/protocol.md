# Deposit gravity, clocks and lensing: specified test

Use a positive Plummer density with prescribed deposited effective mass m(t), scale a, and potential Phi=-G*m(t)/sqrt(r*r+a*a). This is known gravitational mathematics, not a derived capture profile. Conditional response rho_effective=u_deposit/c0^2 assumes ordinary weak-field gravitational coupling; alternative amplification requires another explicit rule. Adopt the diagnostic metric ds^2=-exp(2phi)c0^2dt^2+exp(-2gamma*phi)dx^2 with phi=Phi/c0^2. gamma=0 preserves the prior fixed spatial rods; gamma=1 has the ordinary equal-potential weak-field limit. The exponential form is a chosen completion, not an exact Einstein solution for growing Plummer matter. No homogeneous expansion is inserted. gamma=1 changes local spatial geometry explicitly.

In units a=c0=G=1, let phi(x,t)=-beta*(1+f*t/20)/sqrt(1+x*x), beta=1e-6,1e-5, f=0,0.1,1, gamma=0,1. Integrate central radial rays x=-10..10 emitted at t=0 and neighboring emissions +/-0.001. These are 12 prescribed histories, not observation fits. Compare S=J*exp(phi_o-phi_e), with dt/dx=exp[-(1+gamma)phi] and dlogJ/dx=partial_t exp[-(1+gamma)phi], against independently integrated endpoint proper-clock intervals to 1e-7. Compare growing cases against the first-order expression logS=beta*f*[(1+gamma)*asinh(10)/10-1/sqrt(101)], recording approximation error, not asserting exact equality.

At mass epoch t=0 derive circular speed at r=a, static Born deflection at impact a, and gravitational binding energy by independent quadratures. Verify vc^2/c0^2=beta/(2sqrt2), deflection=(1+gamma)*beta radians, and U=-3*pi*G*m^2/(32a) to 1e-9 relative. These instantaneous weak-field diagnostics do not solve a time-dependent orbit, full bent ray or capture formation history. Record all assumptions and do not import a halo mass fit as a derivation of deposits.
