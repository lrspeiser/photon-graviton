# Response of a small continuously injected radial population

Before computation: use p6,C1,kappa0=.1,R100,c/v0=1000, whose sampled cold source was initially bound. Reconstruct the instantaneous absorbed source and radial merger velocity. Follow a negligible added rest-mass population in the frozen ordinary-plus-deposit potential; this is the linear response kernel, not a full formation calculation. Original deposits are background only, not simultaneously assumed to obey this new source dynamics. Source shape is held fixed. New particles pass through the center on signed radial trajectories; no sticking, reflection wall, cooling, torque, or removal is imposed.

For constant injection, integrate trajectories over ages0..T at T=1,10,30 in units a/v0. Compare normalized added mass within r=.1,1,3,10 against permanent deposition at the injection position. Compute mean added kinetic and mechanical energies. Coarse:512-shell field and source,2 launch nodes per shell,dt=.004. Time check: same spatial resolution,dt=.002. Fine:1024-shell field and source,4 launch nodes per shell,dt=.002. Require each enclosed mass fraction to change by less than0.005 absolute and mean kinetic energy by less than1 percent. Require maximum single-orbit energy change less than1e-4 in G M_b/a units. Source mass accounting follows normalized age weights and must close within1e-10 relative. Retain and refine failures. No observational data or self-gravity of the added population is included; do not claim a supported finite-mass galaxy has formed.

Before adding the projection calculation: also integrate the projected enclosed fraction of each spherical shell at impact radii.1,1,3,10, using fraction1-sqrt(1-b^2/r^2) for r>b and1 otherwise. Apply the same0.005 absolute refinement gate. This geometrical mass projection provides a conditional lensing input, not a lensing observation fit.
