# Pure clock-rate geometry consistency check

Freeze before execution. This is an optional general-relativity consistency comparison, not adoption of an expanding universe or a requirement that GR be the final theory. Use fixed flat spatial geometry, zero shift, and positive lapse q(t,x). Local proper clocks have d tau=q dt and null rays have local speed c0. Units c0=1; no cosmological constant.

Established metric form: diag(-q^2,1,1,1). Compute the Christoffel symbols, Ricci tensor, scalar curvature and Einstein tensor symbolically from their definitions. Do not infer matter support from the ray equation alone. Compare against the standard spatial-flat, zero-extrinsic-curvature Hamiltonian constraint, which requires zero normal-observer energy density in GR.

Use illustrative q=1-0.1 exp(-x^2) at a fixed time only to display the sign of transverse stresses at x=0 and x=2. The profile is prescribed, not generated by companion energy and not a fit. Examine whether the usual null energy condition holds. This one-dimensional spatial profile is a planar diagnostic, not a finite spherical void model.

Provenance: metric, curvature definitions, Einstein equation and energy-condition test are established physics. Application and symbolic checks are performed here; no uniqueness claim. A failure applies to the exact flat-spatial pure-lapse ansatz under GR, not all nonexpanding or time-field models. Curved nonexpanding spatial geometry, different gravitational equations and a separate interaction require distinct tests.
