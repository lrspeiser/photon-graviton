# JR-1 amendment 4: one universal temporal/spatial response ratio

21 September 2026. Post-transfer exploratory construction. R0-R10 and the original R9 transfer record remain unchanged. R10 gives fitted stellar fractional RMS 1.563 percent and lens-angle RMS 5.257 percent; on the two optimizer-excluded but now exposed lenses these are 2.660 percent and 11.567 percent. It does not establish observational validity.

The user's authorization permits physical parameters and assumptions to change. The equal-potential response was a hypothesis, not an observed identity for a proposed companion sector. R11 relaxes that hypothesis with ONE universal gamma_chi:

    U_t = Phi_b + chi
    U_s = Phi_b + gamma_chi * chi.

Massive-body motion still uses g_b+g_chi. Weak-field ray deflection now uses g_b + (1+gamma_chi)*g_chi/2 in the existing four-over-c-squared integral. Thus the same computed companion field supplies both responses through a single shared coefficient. It is not an independent companion profile or a per-object lensing multiplier.

Gamma_chi is bounded [0,3], initialized at 1, without a fitted or added lens-error floor. All source, stellar-population, orbit, distance and observational conventions remain those of R10. The 21-parameter fit uses only the original 89 SPARC training galaxies and four fit lenses; the other scores are post-fit development checks and cannot be called blind validation after the previous exposure.

This explicitly changes the original equal-potential/no-extra-photon-coefficient assumption. A measured fit coefficient is NOT a microscopic derivation of two potentials, a relativistic action, a common gravitational-wave cone or an acceptable Solar-system response. It is an effective universal coupling to be connected to such a construction. The same-field condition does not by itself enforce gamma_chi=1; universal coupling is different from independently tuning each lens. Do not conceal the extra parameter or compare it to R10 as though the models had equal flexibility.
