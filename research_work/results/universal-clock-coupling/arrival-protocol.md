# Arrival retention and static endpoint-region check

Extend the five previously tested prescribed q profiles with a downstream static region at x=3..4 and a detector at x=6. Use static contrasts 0, +0.2 and -0.2, and launch times 0 and 2: thirty fixed cases. Keep q=1 at emission, between regions and at the detector. Do not fit any physical parameter or claim an autonomous companion reservoir.

Integrate photon momentum and reference energy independently using Hamilton's equations for H=q c p, positive p and c=1. Track local energy H/q, reference work from partial time dependence, and arrival-time sensitivity. Save checkpoints before/inside/after the static region. Compare the net shift with the original matching case, verify reference energy remains constant downstream of the changing region, and repeat with tighter integration tolerances. Local energy inside a static clock contrast may change; do not label that a reversal of the accumulated matched-endpoint shift.

Any complementary quantity defined as initial photon energy minus current reference energy is a bookkeeping work account only. It is not evidence of creation, transport, no-loss propagation or deposition of a companion field. Preserve this limitation in the report and paper framing.
