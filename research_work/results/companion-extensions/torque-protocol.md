# Torque and cooling budgets

Freeze shared settling and orbital profiles. For moved mass initially at 0.1-30 kpc calculate scalar angular-momentum reductions shell by shell. Direct freely escaping massless ray emission from within the source radius obeys |L_orb|<=r E/c, so calculate the minimum energy needed for this route and compare with the earlier properly counted total cooling budget. This is not a universal bound on coherent wave spin/multipole radiation, internal torques or reabsorbed radiation. Summed magnitudes are a requirement for separately braking shells, not the net vector angular momentum of the isotropic system.

For alternative torque receivers, use existing unshifted companion shells at 15-30, 30-60 and 60-120 kpc. Distribute the scalar angular-momentum load equally per unit receiver mass, with matched orientations understood as a best-case construction. Compute the initial first-order work integral Omega dJ and the finite test-particle orbit-energy/radius change in the frozen final potential. These are feasibility diagnostics, not self-consistent receiving profiles, transport rates or a derived interaction. Retain all receiver choices; do not choose by speed agreement. Check numerical refinement and use global cooling only as a budget comparator, not a replacement for recomputing the system's self-energy after actual redistribution.
