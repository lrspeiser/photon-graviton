# Correction: fitted normalization versus physical density amplitude

13 September 2026. Discovered while preparing the new capacity branches for lensing.

## Error and correction

The original exact-third fit stores C0=4.728589242e7 Msun/kpc^3 under `C_Msun_kpc3`, but defines its full pre-retention amplitude as A=2 C0=9.457178483e7 under `source_C_before_retention_Msun_kpc3`. Its actual density is A eta(X) J g. Both `isotropic-galaxy-transfer/run.py` and `lensing.py` implement this factor. Main-paper v1.1 equation (7) also states it correctly.

The recent capture-capacity audit used C0 where its absolute capacity formulas required A. The conservative-recycling diagnostic made the same mistake only in its reported total deposited masses. Those absolute masses were underestimated by a factor of two. The cause is a parameter-convention error, not a new effect of companion physics.

Both executables now read the explicit pre-retention source amplitude and assert its relation to C0. Density/rate notation in the recent local-capacity, occupancy-transport, conservative-recycling and local-formation reports is clarified: their C means A, while C/original C amplitude ratios are also equal to the corresponding C0 ratios.

## What changes

- Capture-capacity and unattenuated-capacity masses double. The former spans 1.13548e8 to 6.48280e13 Msun equivalent over the full hypothetical profiles.
- The capture-area/capacity ratio halves, to 2.156064843e-9 kpc^2/Msun for the reference-capacity interpretation.
- Reported conservative-recycling total masses double.
- Absolute supply/rate expressions use A in the denominator where they formerly named C ambiguously. They still do not give physical watts without the source normalization and microscopic rate.

These are model capacities and full-profile model masses, not observed masses. The original stellar and galaxy data are unchanged.

## What does not change, and why

The new rotation predictions were generated by multiplying the original saved extra-gravity term by enclosed-mass ratios. The common factor of two cancels from those ratios; the saved baseline already contains the correct original normalization. Consequently all reported rotation predictions, RMSE scores and relative fitted amplitude adjustments remain unchanged. The area/capacity cancellation with galaxy size also remains exact, and the alternative capacity ratio range 0.49454-0.98062 is unchanged.

The original six-lens comparison and the main PDF do not need a factor-of-two correction: they used 2 C0 eta from the outset. This audit does not recalculate or validate their conditional geometry, luminosity proxies or lensing results. The intended transfer of new local-capacity profiles to lensing remains future work; correcting the normalization is necessary preparation, not that comparison's completion.
