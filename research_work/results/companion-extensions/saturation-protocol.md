# Ordered saturation endpoint test

Keep the exact-third inventory and phenomenological donor selection. Replace uniform contraction with an ordered spherical-shell packing prescription: each donor shell retains its mass and shrinks its volume to min(original volume, donor mass / saturation density). Empty shells retain their volume. Initially denser shells are not expanded or compressed internally. Preserve radial ordering and recalculate receiver/unforced circular radii using the coupled solver.

This is a proposed endpoint closure, not a derived local transport equation or total-density ceiling. Calibrate the one saturation density to the previous mass-weighted contraction target s=0.803193303 under baseline I, without optimizing velocities. Freeze it for baseline II and all three receiver bands. Compare original and uniformly contracted controls already present in the coupled results. Check doubled radial resolution without recalibrating the threshold. Keep all results, including nonconvergence, unfavorable speed errors or energy deficits.

Packing is based on donor density alone. Other components can share the volume. Empty volume preservation and shell ordering are additional assumptions to disclose. The test does not prove that a physical torque can form this profile, that saturation provides radial support, or that it explains the one-third retention exponent.
