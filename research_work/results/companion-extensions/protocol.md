# Extensions preserving photon-companion capture: analysis protocol

Preserve empirical alpha, photon energy transfer, traveling companions, deposited energy and the exact-one-third retention law. Do not replace them with a conventional dark halo or adopt expansion. Distinguish imported mathematics from proposed couplings. No claim of novelty or microscopic derivation follows from borrowing a field equation.

A. Axisymmetric refraction pilot. Keep the exact-third deposited density fixed. Solve div(epsilon grad Phi)=4piG rho using a conservative finite-volume correction to the known Newtonian potential. rho contains archived ordinary stars, a spherical thin-gas monopole, and spherical companions. Stars retain their flattened Miyamoto-Nagai/Plummer form. Gas monopole is a declared computational approximation, not the previous full gas-disk force; compare against the SAME epsilon=1 control. epsilon=epsilon_inf+(1-epsilon_inf)*rho_driver/(rho_driver+rho_c), with rho_driver either ordinary density or companion density. Test epsilon_inf=[0.7,0.85,1,1.15,1.3], rho_c=[1e5,1e6,1e7] Msun/kpc^3. Values above one are a proposed weakening-response extension, not standard refracted gravity. No response parameter changes the deposited mass.

Use both MW ordinary-matter baselines. Fit/select grid parameters using only the earlier 20 inner circular-speed bins; evaluate the 18 outer bins and report all cases. All data previously inspected; not blind validation. Report vertical pull at z=1.1 kpc from the same potential, with the prior 43 model-dependent summaries as provisional diagnostics only. Domain R,z<=160 kpc, radial/vertical stretched grid 128x128; selected models/control refined to 192x192 and domain 240 kpc. Boundary is monopole-like Phi_N/epsilon_inf. Verify constant-epsilon identity, sparse residuals, no-refraction control, domain/grid sensitivity. If the pilot is numerically sensitive, report that and do not promote its ranking to a precise result. No lensing prediction follows without a relativistic completion.

B. Ideal-boson phase pilot. Use the same MW capture inventory and local density, with sigma^2=G[M_b,monopole(<r)+M_dep(<r)]/(3r) as an assumed kinetic scale, not a measured companion temperature. For effective massive bosons, T=m sigma^2/kB; Tc=2pi hbar^2/(m kB)*(n/zeta(3/2))^(2/3), n=rho/m. Ideal-gas condensed fraction f=max(0,1-(T/Tc)^(3/2)). Move this fraction to s*r and leave the remainder extended. This combines known ideal Bose-gas thermodynamics with proposed companion mass, kinetic closure and contraction; it is not a solved gravitating condensate. Fit m in 10^-3..10^2 eV/c^2 (41 values), s=0.03..1 (33) on inner bins only; include m=1e-22 eV/c^2 as a separate limit. Retain all outer predictions, total inventory and boundaries. No full stability, self-consistent temperature or energy-loss mechanism is asserted.

C. Conversion diagnostics. Compare ordinary whole-photon conversion with fractional-energy loss; show survivor frequency and energy-accounting distinction. Evaluate a Poisson small-inelastic-loss model with mean redshift fixed to archived alpha. Compute the spectral broadening implied by a per-event fractional loss and illustrative linewidth tolerances; these tolerances are design cases, not newly measured constraints. Include event-duration and momentum/conservation requirements. Do not represent a general mixing equation or a fitted alpha as a microscopic derivation.

D. Effective theory synthesis. Assess QED-inspired couplings, scalar/axion-like fields, refracted response and phase physics as optional MODULES with shared energy/momentum bookkeeping. String theory is possible motivation for a chosen effective field, not a numerical halo prediction. Record necessary missing inputs rather than inventing a unique string model. Summarize prior support/exchange results alongside new calculations and identify which conclusions survive control, transfer and physical limitations.
