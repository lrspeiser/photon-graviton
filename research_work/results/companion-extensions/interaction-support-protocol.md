# Shared pressure law and equilibrium test

Use frozen settled profiles from local-support-results.json. Test total effective polytropic support P=rho_ref*v0^2*(rho/rho_ref)^gamma with rho_ref=1e7 Msun/kpc^3 and gamma=4/3,5/3,2. These are known polytropic forms, proposed as effective companion support; gamma=2 is motivated by repulsive contact-interaction condensates. Fit one v0 per gamma to log required pressure at 100 log-spaced radii 0.1-10 kpc, equal-weighted between both ordinary-matter baselines. Diagnose the extra-pressure residual separately; do not call a total-pressure fit a measured interaction. Report pressure errors at 10-30 kpc without using them to choose v0. These are previously seen derived profiles, not new observations.

Then replace the imposed profile by the hydrostatic solution of the chosen total pressure law with the same spherical-average external force and original exact-third total mass. Solve dH/dr=-g_b-GM/r^2 and dM/dr=4pi*r^2*rho(H) from a regular center to zero enthalpy. Scan central density 1e2-1e13 Msun/kpc^3 with 48 points; bracket all mass-matching roots. Retain all finite-radius solutions, no selection by observed speeds. If no root exists within this domain, report the limitation. Compute circular speeds with the same flattened stellar/gas-monopole baseline and spherical new deposit. Check no-external-field gamma=2 against its analytic Lane-Emden solution, root mass accuracy and refined solver tolerances.

These equilibrium cases replace the phase-selector closure, not the photon-source concept or one-third inventory. They establish neither formation nor full stability nor temperature/energy consistency. Cooling and pressure-source energy remain additional requirements. A density that increases outward cannot satisfy isotropic hydrostatic equilibrium with any everywhere positive-compressibility barotropic pressure; check this before interpreting fit residuals.
