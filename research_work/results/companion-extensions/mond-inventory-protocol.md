# MOND-guided fixed-inventory redistribution

Declared before execution, 13 September 2026. Use all 149 saved SPARC galaxies and 3150 accepted radii, with original 89/29/31 splits and ordinary-matter baselines. No new observations, sample exclusions or changes to mass-to-light ratios. All data previously exposed. Keep the exact-third capture constants and retention factor. Fix a0 to the previously training-fitted simple MOND value, not a new optimized value.

At each measured radius calculate g_target=MOND_simple(g_b)-g_b and spherical equivalent M_target=r^2*g_target/G. Record every declining adjacent target-mass interval: these require negative shell density under a spherical deposit interpretation, although the original baryonic disk is not spherical. Do not claim algebraic MOND is a full 3D gravity solution.

Construct a nonnegative diagnostic endpoint by the deterministic running maximum of target enclosed mass (ordered radius), capped at the total reference companion inventory. This is an explicit modification of MOND, not a density derived from transport. Its positive piecewise-linear enclosed-mass interpolation has nonnegative shell mass. All remaining inventory can be assigned outside the final measured radius, for example between 2 and 3 times that radius. That outer completion is arbitrary and untested by lensing, not a physical cutoff or stopping law. No mass is created or discarded; existing material can move both inward and outward, not solely migrate inward.

Predict M_new(<r)=(1-f)M_reference(<r)+f*M_endpoint(<r), f shared in [0,1]. Fit f on training mean equal-galaxy squared log10 speed error, scan 101 values and refine interior minima. Freeze it for validation/test. Report unmodified companion, raw algebraic MOND, full endpoint, and selected mixture. Repeat inventory quadrature with doubled angles. Preserve mass-cap counts, monotonicity failures and all prediction rows. No clipping negative density without reporting the original violation. Do not fit to the old NFW profiles or refit this rule to validation/test.

This uses three inherited capture constants, one inherited fitted MOND scale and one new mixture parameter. A successful speed fit would test achievable redistribution at fixed inventory, not derive capture or prove source energy, lensing, vertical gravity, orbital support, release energy or dynamical stability. Report the original MOND provenance and the hypothetical interpretation explicitly.
