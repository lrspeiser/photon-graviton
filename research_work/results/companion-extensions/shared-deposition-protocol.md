# Shared effective deposition rule

Declared before fitting. Six exposed NFW halo fits are inverse targets, not measured deposits. Freeze their stellar/orbital calibration for observational replay. Use original population-mass proxies (not the halo-fitted stellar mass), half-light size Re and archived exact-third eta as predictors. Define W=G Mpop/Re and Wstar=(300 km/s)^2. Propose NFW-shaped effective deposited density with amplitude A=K eta Mpop (W/Wstar)^p and scale rs=b Re (W/Wstar)^q. NFW is established mathematics; the shared population/depth scaling and companion interpretation are hypothetical, not microscopic derivations. This is a test of integrated capture times retention, not a specified incoming flux or permanent-storage history.

Test nested two-, three-, and four-parameter families: p=q=0; free p with q=0; free p,q. Bounds K=1e-4..1e5, b=0.01..1000, p,q=-4..4. Fit equal-galaxy mean squared differences of asinh(Mhalo/Mpop), at observed annular midpoint radii, between shared rule and frozen NFW targets. This admits the zero-halo system without deleting it or taking log zero. Fixed multistarts; keep convergence and boundary flags. Fit all six for description, then six leave-one-out fits per family and freeze each omitted prediction. Do not choose fresh bounds or remove systems after seeing the result.

Replay stellar motions and lens bending with ordinary-matter and orbit parameters frozen and no lens recalibration. Report all families and total motion chi-squared plus lens fractional discrepancies. A full-sample fit is descriptive; omitted-system fits are exposed-sample transfer diagnostics, not blind validation. The independent halo targets have weakly constrained scales and boundary fits; no probability or exclusion of all shared laws follows. NFW tail, energy supply, transport attenuation, stable support and a physical outer boundary remain unresolved even if inverse fits improve.
