# Single-parameter size/retention test

Declared before execution, 13 September 2026. All targets have been exposed.
Test rho_s(r)=s^-2 rho_reference(r/s), hence M_s(<r)=s M_reference(<r/s).
Use one shared s in [0.5,1], with unchanged one-third exponent, reference
normalization, opacity shape and ordinary-matter contributions. This is a
transformation of the deposited profile, not re-solving the opacity with a new
physical interaction. At s=1 recover the reference; total stored mass is s times
reference. The remainder is unretained energy in this bookkeeping branch, not
deleted energy or a demonstrated escape process.

Fit only s on the original 89 training galaxies using the original equal-galaxy
mean squared log10 speed objective. Scan 101 equally spaced values and polish
each detected local minimum, including both endpoints. Freeze s for all 29
validation and 31 test galaxies. Recompute the reference in the same integration
and check against saved predictions. Double radial/angular resolution; compare
frozen-s scores and report boundary behavior. Do not enlarge the range after
seeing results. Report original MOND/ordinary/NFW comparators separately.

Record per-galaxy/radius predictions, signed radial-bin errors, speed and log
RMSE, total stored fraction and per-galaxy improvement counts. No new target
parameters, data cuts, Bayesian evidence or untouched-sample claim. Do not adopt
the modification as a replacement unless improvements transfer; lensing,
support and a physical energy destination remain separate requirements.
