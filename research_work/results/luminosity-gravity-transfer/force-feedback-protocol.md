# Accumulated capture responding to total force

Declared before results. Alternative to binding-depth capture: d rho_d(r,s)/ds=C[g_tot(r,s)/(g_tot(r,s)+g0)]^2, rho_d(r,0)=0, s=0..1 normalized accumulated exposure; g_tot=g_b+G M_d(<r,s)/r^2. Fixed exponent 2; fit common log10 C in [-2,12] Msun/kpc^3 and log10 g0 in [-2,7] (km/s)^2/kpc. Starts (7,3),(5,2),(9,4). Compare with feedback switched off (g_tot replaced by g_b), fitting each separately on the same 89 training galaxies. Freeze for original 29/31 evaluation groups. Same equal-galaxy log-speed objective and ordinary-matter templates as prior protocol. No galaxy-specific parameters.

This is a distinct postulate responding to local force magnitude, not the previous absolute-depth law. The squared saturating function is not a known microphysical derivation or a novelty claim. Known spherical Newtonian integration updates the actual accumulated density throughout exposure; do not substitute rho=C f(g_final), which would erase formation history. C=(kappa_max/c) integral u_c dt remains an unknown common exposure, not a universe age or calculated photon supply. In the optically thin isotropic bath companions lose energy only by capture; deposited density is retained. Attenuation, source depletion, orbital redistribution/support and nonspherical feedback are still absent. A preferred-frame/environmental force response would require a physical definition beyond this Newtonian surrogate.

Use 256 radial intervals per galaxy to its last observed radius and 32 midpoint exposure steps. Interpolate ordinary acceleration linearly from zero at the origin through the measured baryonic templates. All final fitted models are checked at 512 intervals/64 steps, with <0.1 km/s change in group RMS required for reporting the coarse fit as stable. If it fails, retain the failure and fit at the finer settings. This is a necessary prediction check, not a data-resolution investigation. No physical capture edge is asserted; a spherical exterior cannot change interior force, but global finite-mass existence is not established by this interior computation.

Report parameters, bounds, all starts, frozen group scores, per-galaxy predictions and feedback comparison. Existing partitions are exposed; this is exploratory model testing, not blind validation. All six goals remain open.
