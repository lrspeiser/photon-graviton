# Shared external-bath capture on actual galaxies

Before fitting: test a positive optically thin deposited-density rule rho_d(r)=C[W_b(r)/(W_b(r)+W0)]^4. W_b=-Phi_b is a spherical proxy for ordinary binding depth with Phi_b(infinity)=0. Retain the observed gas/disk/bulge radial acceleration used previously. Integrate that acceleration outward, using linear interpolation between observed radii, g proportional to r inside the first radius, and a Kepler continuation outside the final point. This is a stated spherical proxy for disk geometry, not a full disk potential reconstruction.

The exponent 4 is fixed to make the mass integral converge in the Kepler tail (rho proportional to r^-4). Fit common C in [10^-2,10^12] Msun/kpc^3 and W0 in [10,10^7] (km/s)^2 on the 89 original training galaxies; three starts (log10 C,log10 W0)=(7,4),(5,3),(9,5). Equal-galaxy log10 speed objective, original 29/31 evaluation partitions, no per-galaxy amplitude. Compute M_d(<r)=4pi integral rho_d(s)s^2 ds and v_pred^2=v_b^2+GM_d(<r)/r. Compare archived empirical scores, reporting failures and parameter boundaries.

Known transport bookkeeping motivates C=(kappa_max/c) integral u_c(t)dt for stationary undepleted isotropic energy density u_c and capture coefficient kappa=kappa_max[W_b/(W_b+W0)]^4. The saturating depth dependence is a postulate, not derived microphysics or a claim of novelty. C is fitted exposure, not a calculated stellar energy supply or universe age. Companions are lost only by capture. The deposited profile assumes retention/support and neglects self-gravity feedback in the capture rate, attenuation and focusing; enforce none of these by assertion. Report whether inferred extra force makes neglect of feedback questionable. Profiles have vanishing capture as W approaches zero. No arbitrary physical outer edge is imposed. The integration grid beyond data affects only diagnostics, not an invented universe boundary.

Input assumptions and exposed-split limitations remain as in protocol.md. This is a necessary profile-shape test of a restricted physical candidate, not a completed self-consistent capture solution. All six research goals stay open.
