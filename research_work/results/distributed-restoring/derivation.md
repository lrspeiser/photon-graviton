# Restoring potential removes indefinite homogeneous rolling

## Provenance and scope

The positive quadratic scalar potential is an established field-theory form. Its use here is an optional extension of this project's proposed optical coupling. The following reductions and turning-point expressions are derived here within that model; originality is unverified. Energy conservation and one-dimensional conservative-motion methods are established mathematics, not novelty claims.

Keep the fixed finite periodic homogeneous sector with n=1+g phi and initial radiation density U0. Add the established potential V(phi)=K m^2 phi^2/2. No expansion, source replenishment, dissipation or deposited-energy reservoir is supplied. The reference-clock and matter-coupling limitations of the earlier zero-mode calculation remain.

Derived here within the proposed model; originality unverified:

n_ddot = A/n^2 - m^2(n-1), where A=g^2 U0/K.

Established energy conservation, applied to this model:

K/(2g^2) [n_dot^2 + m^2(n-1)^2] + U0/n = U0.

At a turning point with n greater than one, cancel n-1 in that energy equation. Derived here; originality unverified:

n_max = [1 + sqrt(1+8A/m^2)]/2, for m>0.

The potential energy grows quadratically at large n, so finite energy forbids unbounded rolling for every positive m in this closed homogeneous model. The system oscillates between n=1 and n_max. At its return to n=1 the radiation has recovered its initial energy and the field has zero kinetic and restoring energy. This is a reversal of energy exchange within the same bath, not disappearance of previously observed photon shifts after those photons have left a different system.

Established ray identity applied to the candidate: alpha=n_dot/c0. Therefore the instantaneous accumulation rate changes sign. For a probe spanning a full field cycle with matching field values at its endpoints, the established integrated identity gives unit reference-frequency ratio. Intervals covering only part of the cycle can redshift or blueshift. Matter clocks are not solved here, so these are not certified atomic spectral predictions.

## Consequence for the environmental proposal

A restoring term confined to dense regions might coexist with a nearly flat field potential in voids; this uniform calculation does not test that geometry. However, assigning a zero void potential solely because it permits the desired effect is a new assumption requiring a coupling and stability explanation. The duration needed before a turn depends on m, A and initial state; no physical bound in years is possible without normalization. The m-to-zero limit and infinite-time limit differ: a small m can allow a long transient, but finite positive m cannot support indefinite rolling in this model.

Continuous replenishment, a different potential, spatial energy transport or a new interaction would change the problem and must carry their own source budget and equations. Do not add damping or a driver only to remove the reversal while omitting the recipient or fuel energy. This result is a constraint on the present optional closed-field candidate, not a general no-go theorem for all nonexpanding redshift mechanisms.
