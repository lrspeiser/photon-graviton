# BRIDGE-1B: one interaction for the photon's loss and the companions' mass

Declared before execution, 15 September 2026. Baseline: `main` at 674d4c7.

**Why it is run.** The project owner's proposal of 15 September: make the shared interaction, not another halo fit, the research target. Their reading of the repository is that the propagation field which changes light's frequency should also produce the companions, and should then feel their back-reaction, instead of a redshift rate, a production rate and a daughter speed being chosen independently. They built a finite-mode prototype, found that the energy chain closes but that its products are hot, that today's radiation power is about 2.75 million times short of 2B-F1's source power, and that a simple drag on the rolling field moves the brightness prediction the wrong way. They asked for the next cycle to attack two milestones: **a physical companion source** and **physical optical predictions**, keeping RC-2a as the downstream transport test.

**The owner's package.** The prototype was offered as a sandbox download that this session cannot read. This protocol is an independent implementation from the owner's description, so its code-unit numbers are not expected to match theirs run for run. If the package is shared through the repository, the comparison is added then.

**What a feasibility pass already showed, before this declaration.** The mode integrator, the mapping and the brightness pipeline were prototyped to size the tolerances and the scan ranges below; the bracketed numbers in part A are what the prototype reached, and the brightness pipeline reproduced PF-1's archived χ² = 871.55 exactly. That pass also indicated the qualitative answers (cold products, a photon-supplied fraction of order 10⁻⁶ unless the history begins hot, and a brightness cost that falls with the field's stored energy). The gates below are set from the physics and from the archived comparisons, not from those outcomes, and every number in the report is regenerated by the declared code.

**The one rule.** There is a single accounting of the photon's loss. Radiation gives energy to the field through the matched-wave exchange, the field gives energy to the companions, and nothing adds a second photon-to-companion conversion for the same energy. This is the bound-pair work's warning (`bound-pair-production/derivation.md`, line 126) applied to the bridge.

## What is fixed, and what is derived

**Fixed inputs.**
- **The wave sector.** The matched-wave action (`matched-wave/derivation.md`): ε = μ = n, so photon energy density in a fixed volume is u_γ = A/n, and the exchange is u̇_γ = −(ṅ/n)u_γ. PF-1's archived rate is the normalization: ṅ/n today = cα = 74.618 km/s/Mpc = 2.4180×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹ (1/h = 13.10 Gyr), with n today = 1.
- **The radiation.** u_γ,0 = 4.1747×10⁻¹⁴ J/m³ (a = 7.565723×10⁻¹⁶ J m⁻³ K⁻⁴, T = 2.72548 K), so A = u_γ,0.
- **The companion benchmark, a comparison scale and not a requirement.** 2B-F1's reference source, as the revision selected it: 3 km/s, collisionless, with the bath's gravity on, q\* = 1,437.8 M☉ kpc⁻³ Gyr⁻¹ over 10 Gyr, so ρ_C,0 = 1.4378×10⁴ M☉ kpc⁻³ = 2.095×10⁶ u_γ,0/c² ≈ 106 times the critical density for H₀ = 70. RC-2a stage 1 showed that this rate was selected by a 300 kpc zone, so it fixes a scale here, not a target to hit.
- **Brightness.** Pantheon+SH0ES as PF-1's T4 used it: the same 960 rows with zHD ≥ 0.1, the same full covariance, the same Cepheid-only calibration, one free offset per model. PF-1's coasting law scores χ² = 871.55 there and flat FLRW (Ω_m = 0.3) 836.51.
- **The clock bound.** Lange et al. (2021), Yb⁺ E3 / Cs: −3.1(3.4)×10⁻¹⁷ per year.

**Derived, not chosen:** the companions' momentum spectrum, their number, their mass history, their speed at every epoch, the production rate as a function of time, the field's deceleration, and the redshift history that follows.

## The model

In a homogeneous closed volume, with ħ = c = 1 in the frame the matched-wave action selects:

    L = K ṅ²/2 − V(n) + [wave modes: Σ (n Ȧ_k²/2 − k²A_k²/(2n))] + ∫d³x [χ̇²/2 − |∇χ|²/2 − m_C²(n) χ²/2],
    m_C²(n) = m0² + g²(n − n*)².

- **n** is classical. **χ**, the companion field, is a free quantum field in a Gaussian state, started in the adiabatic vacuum.
- **Modes.** ω_k² = k² + m_C²(n). Each mode is integrated in the adiabatic (Bogoliubov) basis: α̇_k = (ω̇_k/2ω_k)e^{2iθ}β_k, β̇_k = (ω̇_k/2ω_k)e^{−2iθ}α_k, θ̇ = ω_k, starting at α = 1, β = 0. The adiabatic particle number is n_k = |β_k|².
- **Back-reaction.** K n̈ = A/n² − V′(n) − g²(n − n*) S, with S = Σ_j w_j (|β_j|² + Re(α_jβ_j*e^{−2iθ_j}))/ω_j and isotropic quadrature weights w_j = k_j²Δk/2π².
- **Energy.** E = Kṅ²/2 + V(n) + A/n + ρ_C, ρ_C = Σ_j w_j ω_j |β_j|². The equations conserve E exactly; the vacuum's own n-dependence (Σ ω_k/2) is subtracted, which is the statement that **V(n) is the renormalized potential**. The size of the term that renormalization absorbs is reported as a diagnostic, not hidden: the one-loop m_C⁴(n)/64π² scale against the field's kinetic energy.
- **What this is not.** It is not a renormalized continuum field theory, it has no spatial dynamics for n, no companion self-interaction, no matter sector, and no gravity. Those are named under scope.

## Calculation 1: the production spectrum, with feedback

### Part A, code-unit trials (declared tolerances)

A feasibility prototype sized these tolerances before the declaration; the numbers in brackets are what it reached.

1. **V1, the crossing spectrum.** A prescribed crossing n = n* + ṅ*t, no back-reaction: n_k must match exp[−π(k² + m0²)/(g|ṅ*|)] to 10⁻³ absolute and 2% relative wherever n_k ≥ 10⁻² [3.1×10⁻⁴, 0.22%].
2. **V2, energy.** |ΔE|/E ≤ 10⁻⁸ in every trial [1.5×10⁻¹¹].
3. **V3, the back-reaction and trapping.** With V = 0, no radiation and the field entering the crossing at ṅ*, the field stops at Δn_trap, which must match K ṅ*²/(2N g) = 4π³ K ṅ*^{1/2} e^{πm0²/(gṅ*)} g^{−5/2} to 5% [0.52%], and the companions' energy at that point must equal the field's initial kinetic energy to 10⁻⁸ [exact to rounding].
4. **V4, momentum and cooling.** After the crossing, every n_k is constant to 10⁻³ and the companions' r.m.s. speed follows k_rms/m_C(n) to 1%, with k_rms = √(3/2π)·k\*, k\*² = g|ṅ*|.
5. **V5, the four controls the owner named.**
   - No radiation and a field at rest: |N| ≤ 10⁻⁸ of the reference trial's N.
   - No coupling (g = 0): no companions, and the field's gain equals the radiation's loss.
   - A frozen index: no redshift and no production.
   - A moving field with no radiation: companions are produced, funded by the field's own kinetic energy. This control is the point of R1 below: a shared field is not automatically a photon-powered one.
6. **V6, the probe signals.** Two weak signals propagated between fixed coordinate positions through the computed n(t). The arrival-interval stretch equals the carrier redshift n_o/n_e in the limit of a vanishing emission interval: the deviation must fall by 2.0 ± 0.2 when the interval is halved, and the extrapolated stretch must match n_o/n_e to 10⁻⁷. The optical time is carried as a state of the same integration, so the ray mapping has the solver's own accuracy.
7. **V7, the kinetic hybrid.** Once every populated mode satisfies |ω̇_k|/ω_k² < 10⁻³, the calculation may continue with frozen n_k and the force Σ w_j n_j ∂ω_j/∂n. The switch must move Δn_trap by less than 1%, and the result must not depend on the switching time (two switch times a factor of 3 apart, same tolerance).
8. **The reference trial.** One photon-driven trial (field at rest, radiation the only initial energy) is reported as the owner's was: radiation lost, companion energy, field kinetic energy, r.m.s. speed, and the maximum energy error.

### Part B, the physical mapping

The physical question is what the interaction must be for the companions to exist at the benchmark abundance while n stays the field PF-1 fits. Given (n\*, R_E) the model is fully determined:

- **R_E** is the field's present kinetic energy in units of the companions' present rest energy: Kh²/2 = R_E ρ_C,0c². Scanned over 1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300.
- **n\*** is the index at the crossing, so the crossing lookback time follows from the history. Scanned over 0.9, 0.7, 0.5, 0.3, 0.237 and 0.1. The reference is n\* = 0.237, which puts the crossing 10 Gyr ago, matching the benchmark's span.
- **m0** is taken at zero in the reference (μ0 = m0/k\* = 0) and 0.5 as a variant; it enters only through e^{−πμ0²}.
- **The history.** V = 0. Integrated backwards from today (n = 1, ṅ = h) with the radiation force A/n² and the companion force ∂ρ_C/∂n, which is N g once the companions are non-relativistic. Below n\* the companion force is absent. The benchmark fixes that force without iteration, because N g = ρ_C,0/(1 − n\*); the crossing speed ṅ\* the history returns then fixes the microphysics below.
- **Derived microphysics.** k\*⁵ = 8π³ ṅ\* ρ_C,0 e^{πμ0²}/(1 − n\*) fixes k\*, then g = k\*²/ṅ\*, N = ρ_C,0/[g(1 − n\*)], and m_C,0 = g(1 − n\*).
- **A driven variant.** A linear potential V′(n) = −λ adds a constant force. λ is scanned so that the net acceleration â = (λ − Ng)/(Kh²) covers −0.4 to +1, in the steps 1, 0.5, 0.2, 0.0655 (the companion drain alone at the reference point), 0, −0.05, −0.1, −0.15, −0.2, −0.3, −0.4. It answers whether a potential that funds the companions can also move the brightness the way the Pantheon+ residuals want, and what it must supply. **Selecting â by the Pantheon+ χ² is a one-parameter fit to exposed data**, and it is reported as a fit, against flat FLRW's one-parameter 836.51, never as a prediction.

## Calculation 2: the measured shift, against atoms

For every history from calculation 1, and for PF-1's coasting law as the control:

- **Coordinate quantities.** 1 + z_coord = n_o/n_e, the arrival-interval stretch, and the optical distance D = ∫c dt/n with D_L = (1+z)D.
- **The atomic-reference factor.** 1 + z_measured = (n_o/n_e)(ν_atom,o/ν_atom,e), evaluated in each completion the repository has derived:
  - **M1**, the matched action with fixed charges and masses: atomic optical frequencies ∝ n⁻², so 1 + z_measured = n_e/n_o, a blueshift, and the fine-to-gross ratio drifts.
  - **M2**, fixed electrostatics (ε_r = 1, μ_r = n²): the full shift with fixed gross structure, and hyperfine ∝ n², so the optical-to-hyperfine ratio drifts at −2ṅ/n.
  - **M3**, the matched action with electron mass ∝ n²: the full shift with fixed gross structure, rulers ∝ n⁻¹.
  - **M4**, the universal clock dτ = dt/n: the shift cancels.
  - **M5**, CC-1 co-scaling: the full shift with every clock ratio fixed and ruler counts growing.
- **The gates.** O1, a positive measured shift equal to the history's own n_o/n_e (p = 1); O2, the event stretch equals 1 + z_measured; O3, every clock ratio's present drift within the Yb⁺/Cs bound (|drift| ≤ 10⁻¹⁶ per year, about three times its uncertainty); O4, the number of atomic rulers between fixed galaxies constant. A completion passes only with all four. The largest p that O3 allows is reported as the family's own limit.
- **An identity to state exactly, rather than five cases.** Take the minimal power-law family ε ∝ n^a, μ ∝ n^c, m_e ∝ n^b, e ∝ n^d, with the nucleus and matter's own limiting speed fixed. Then, with p the exponent of the measured shift, 1 + z_measured = (n_o/n_e)^p:
  - p = b + 4d − 2a + (a+c)/2;
  - rulers ∝ n^(a−b−2d), so fixed ruler counts need a = b + 2d;
  - the Coulomb fine-structure constant ∝ n^(2d−a), so a fixed α needs d = a/2;
  - the hyperfine-to-optical ratio ∝ n^(c+8d+2b−3a).

  Fixed rulers and a fixed α force d = a/2 and b = 0, the gross structure is then fixed automatically, and the hyperfine-to-optical ratio drifts at exactly 2p·ṅ/n. The Yb⁺/Cs bound therefore bounds **p itself**, not just one completion: the calculation reports that bound, and where M1–M5 sit in the algebra. This is the sharp form of "a candidate must supply its own atomic-reference factor".
- **Brightness.** For each history, the Pantheon+ χ² under M2's convention (the only completion with the full shift and a fixed gross structure), with one free offset, against PF-1's 871.55 and FLRW's 836.51. Reported for the constant-force family, the driven variant, and the owner's linear-damping approximation, whose distance law D = (c/h)·ln[1 + (1+γ)z]/(1+γ) is checked against their magnitudes at z = 1 (−0.059, −0.274, −0.505 mag at γ = 0.1, 0.5, 1.0) and then scored on the same 960 rows, which the owner's diagnostic did not do.

## Calculation 3: the RC-2a interface — specified here, not run

Deferred by the owner's own order. What the source would hand over, and what RC-2a would have to change:
- one production epoch rather than a rate: t\*, with the momentum distribution exp(−πk²/k\*²) and a number density N;
- a companion mass that keeps changing, m_C(t) = g(n(t) − n\*), so the orbit equations need d(mv)/dt = −m∇Φ, that is a drag −(ṁ/m)v, and the gravitating mass must be the current one;
- the donor's depletion is the field's own energy loss, uniform in space, so RC-2a's "daughters minus depletion" accounting carries over unchanged;
- the energy transfer Q_C = N g ṅ c², tied to the redshift rate rather than free.

## Readings (declared before the run)

- **R1, the energy source.** The photon-supplied fraction f_γ = u_γ,0(1/n\* − 1)/ρ_C,0c² for a history that begins at the crossing with stored kinetic energy; and, for a history that begins at rest, the turnaround index n_s = u_γ,0/[(R_E+1)ρ_C,0c²], its temperature T_0/n_s and its lookback time. Photon-powered means the radiation delivered at least half the companions' energy.
- **R2, the history.** Δμ at z = 0.5, 1 and 1.5 against the coasting law, the Pantheon+ χ², and the smallest R_E whose χ² is within 1 of PF-1's. For the driven variant, the best-fitting â, its χ², and the potential slope λ/Ng and stored energy that â implies.
- **R3, coldness.** v_rms one Gyr after the crossing and today (at the crossing itself the products are relativistic); the distance travelled before falling below 10 km/s and 3 km/s, s(k) = (k/k\*²)·asinh[k\*²(t−t\*)/k].
- **R4, abundance in time.** ρ_C(z)/ρ_C,0 = [1/(1+z) − n\*]/(1 − n\*) at z = 0.5, 1 and 2, and the redshift at which it vanishes.
- **R5, the microphysics.** k\*, g, N, m_C,0, the de Broglie wavelength, and whether the field is trapped (Δn_trap against 1 − n\*).
- **R6, the interface.** ṁ/m today and one Gyr after the crossing (it diverges at the crossing itself, where the mass passes through m0); the production epoch; the momentum spectrum.
- **R7, the renormalization and scope diagnostics.** The one-loop m_C⁴/64π² against Kh²/2; the companions' fifth-force ratio c⁴/[4πG K v_n²(1 − n\*)²] at v_n = c; the local δn a companion overdensity would make at a 200 km/s potential depth.
- **R8, the optical table.** M1–M5 against O1–O4, with each history's present ṅ/n.

## Decision rules

**A physical companion source** passes only if all of:
- **S1, cold enough.** v_rms ≤ 10 km/s within 1 Gyr of the crossing, having travelled less than 1 kpc.
- **S2, abundant without stopping the field.** The benchmark abundance is reached with Δn_trap > 1 − n\*, so n still rolls today.
- **S3, the history survives.** The Pantheon+ χ² is within 1 of PF-1's coasting fit, or better.
- **S4, the supply is named.** R1 states where the energy came from, with its cost: either the radiation's own history, with the required past temperature, or a stored reservoir, with its size.

**Physical optical predictions** pass only if some completion passes O1–O4 with a history that also passes S3.

Anything else is reported as what it is. In particular, if the source passes S1–S3 only for a field carrying thousands of times the critical density, that number is the result, not a footnote.

## Scope: what this does not do

- No spatial dynamics for n: no fifth force computed beyond R7's ratio, no lensing by n gradients, no local back-reaction inside galaxies, no equivalence-principle test.
- No gravity for the homogeneous energies involved. A static background with these densities is the unsolved problem `shared_interaction_test/shared_interaction_report.md` §4 already names.
- No companion self-interaction, no decay, no thermalization, no structure formation. The Jeans instability of a cold uniform medium that 2B-F1 reported applies here too and is not modelled.
- No epoch before the declared start of each history.
- No claim that χ is the companion of the earlier reports, and no fit of anything to rotation curves: calculation 3 is where a source would meet the data, and it is deferred.
- The Pantheon+ comparison inherits every reduction assumption PF-1's T4 lists, and its data are exposed.
