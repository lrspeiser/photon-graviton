# JR-3 reversible channel follow-up

Post-primary exploration, declared after the one-way shared fits preferred zero
extra attenuation and signed per-galaxy inversions showed both directions.
Keep all prior results. Existing comparison data have already been examined;
this is not new blind confirmation.

Use one equal-forward/reverse two-channel energy exchange across an effective
path. Let H(u) be the SAME modeled companion fraction at Re, on the intrinsic
source corrected by u. Incoming companion-to-photon energy flux is X=R*H(u).
For total exchange depth a=tau*H(u), the transmitted photon fraction is

    T = 1 + .5*(X-1)*(1-exp(-a)),
    companion_out = 1 + X - T,
    u*T = 1.

This follows by solving dEgamma/ds=-k Egamma+k Echi and its opposite equation;
total incoming and outgoing energies agree. It is not a photon-specific
lensing factor and does not alter the gravitational law. The influx relation
X=R*H is a declared phenomenological hypothesis, not a derived reservoir flux.
A return flux cannot be supplied forever without energy accounting for its
origin and replenishment; positive static fits do not solve that requirement.

Fit only shared tau in [0,10] and R in [0,9], using 89 training galaxies and the
same equal-object fractional-square objective. Other inputs stay fixed. Use
six fixed starting points. Solve u self-consistently, on [1/(1+R),2], check
all bracketed roots at final parameters and report ambiguity. Evaluate the
archived 29/31 groups without using them to choose another formula.
Compare with the stationary one-box reservoir and do not confuse transport
out of one beam with disappearance of all-direction bolometric luminosity.
The equal-rate limit caps pure one-way removal at 50 percent; that is a feature
of this chosen incoherent channel, not a universal conversion bound.
