# Release arbitrary age and size constraints: conditional first-principles audit

This audit implements the user's instruction to remove outcome-changing assumptions such as a fixed universe age or size, then test the underlying deductions. No universe age, outer radius, Big Bang chronology or expansion is imposed. Infinity is tested as a mathematical limit, not adopted as an observed fact. Conservation and the observational targets remain requirements. Existing data and failed tests are preserved.

**Outcome:** freeing duration removes a universal energy-shortfall claim, but does not derive a photon interaction, event stretching, capture distribution or gravity response. An arbitrarily large/old source population has its own convergence and fuel requirements. These are deductions from explicit simplified postulates, not a completed microscopic first-principles theory.

## 1. Energy without a fixed cosmic age

**Known continuity/integrating-factor mathematics; proposed sector interpretation.** In a declared fixed-volume energy model with consistent time units,

    dU_d/dt = P_cap(t) - lambda(t) U_d,
    U_d(t) = U_d(t0) exp[-integral(t0,t) lambda dv]
           + integral(t0,t) P_cap(u) exp[-integral(u,t) lambda dv] du.

P_cap is net power actually delivered to the deposit, not the luminosity of every possible source counted again for every target. An initial U_d requires a source history; an unknown age does not authorize an arbitrary preloaded halo. Lambda represents energy leaving this reservoir and must feed another counted sector. This linear model does not include uncomputed gravitational binding work.

For constant delivered power P and zero initial deposit:

    lambda=0: U_d=P T.
    lambda=1/tau>0: U_d=P tau (1-exp(-T/tau)).

These are known ODE solutions, not new physical laws. They show exactly why a finite-lifetime deposit cannot be made arbitrarily large just by increasing age: its limiting energy is P tau. A target at that exact limit is only reached asymptotically; a larger target is unreachable within this specialization. Permanent retention allows unlimited growth only if integrated delivered power is itself unlimited.

For a general finite source fuel budget and no initial reservoir or extra driver,

    U_d <= integral P_emitted dt <= E_available_fuel,

assuming positive sector energies and an efficiency at most one. Source recycling cannot debit the same fuel twice. An infinite universe may contain unlimited total fuel, but delivery to a particular finite region must still be calculated.

## 2. Size-free traveling-energy test

**Known spherical-shell geometry and linear transport; explicit diagnostic postulates.** Use a nonexpanding Euclidean background, uniform stationary bolometric emissivity epsilon (power/volume), isotropic sources, constant common travel speed c, fractional photon-energy conversion alpha>0 per length and no companion secondary loss. This is the conversion-only branch with unchanged event rate; it is NOT the shared event-stretching branch. A shell's volume factor r squared cancels inverse-square dilution.

With no capture and sources out to R, the local steady traveling energy densities are

    u_gamma = epsilon/c * (1-exp(-alpha R))/alpha,
    u_c     = epsilon/c * [R-(1-exp(-alpha R))/alpha],
    u_gamma+u_c = epsilon R/c.

Thus the photon integral converges as R tends to infinity while the lossless companion integral diverges. This is traveling energy at a location, not companions being parked in a void. Finite source duration, nonuniform/integrable emissivity, capture or different propagation can change the result. An infinite radius alone does not imply infinite local energy in every possible model.

Introduce coarse-grained capture opacity beta>0 per length, interpreted as interception by wells, not uniform void storage. For a ray's surviving energy fractions,

    dP_gamma/dr=-alpha P_gamma,
    dP_c/dr=alpha P_gamma-beta P_c,
    P_gamma(0)=1, P_c(0)=0.

For beta != alpha:

    P_c = alpha/(beta-alpha) * (exp(-alpha r)-exp(-beta r)).

For beta=alpha, P_c=alpha r exp(-alpha r). The deposit fraction is 1-P_gamma-P_c; it is nonnegative. In the infinite steady-source limit,

    u_gamma=epsilon/(c alpha), u_c=epsilon/(c beta).

The finite traveling density does not close the total steady-state ledger. In the homogeneous corresponding model,

    dot u_gamma=epsilon-c alpha u_gamma,
    dot u_c=c alpha u_gamma-c beta u_c,
    dot u_d=c beta u_c,

the long-time deposition rate approaches epsilon. Permanent deposited energy grows without bound if supply remains constant forever. Finite lifetime can stabilize the deposit sector, but the exported energy needs a destination; the total closed-system energy still increases with injected power. No stable self-gravitating static universe has been derived by this calculation.

## 3. Conclusions that do not depend on universe age or size

**Known arrival-map calculus:** with identical stationary endpoint clocks and a stationary path, t_arr=t_emit+T_path implies dt_arr/dt_emit=1. Making T_path longer does not stretch events. A time-varying environment, different operational endpoint clocks or a nonstationary receiver must supply the additional derivative. The archived nonuniform evolving-clock construction already tests one such possibility; its radiation-generated dynamics remain missing.

**Known continuity and separability:** a fixed source q(x) with permanent deposits gives u_d(x,T)=T q(x). Changing T changes its amplitude, not q(x)/integral q. Orbital redistribution, source evolution and feedback can break separability, but must be calculated rather than attributed automatically to greater age.

**Known parameter degeneracy:** changing deposited energy and inversely changing its free gravitational response can leave the field unchanged. Removing age constraints does not identify the response per joule. In ordinary GR the source includes stresses and momentum as well as energy; this is not a scalar universal cost of curvature. See [Carroll, gravitation and the Newtonian limit](https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/March01/Carroll3/Carroll4.html). No expanding solution is imported.

**Known Newtonian boundary issue:** a uniform sphere of density rho has central potential Phi(0)=-2*pi*G*rho*R squared when zero is defined at infinity. This potential has no finite infinite-radius limit. Local gradients can have different convergence properties. A capture law based on W=-Phi cannot simply inherit this arbitrary outer reference. Under Phi -> Phi+C its rate changes unless W is a specified potential difference or a physically defined additional field. Density, tidal fields or a specified finite escape-energy reference are alternatives to investigate, not adopted repairs. The existing isolated rotating-bar calculations are not invalidated; extrapolating their potential convention to a homogeneous infinite universe would be unjustified.
