Before resolving aliases, take all 14 galaxy identifiers extracted from Table 1 on PDF page 4, plus the explicitly discussed calibration anchor NGC 4258 and Coma object NGC 4874. Resolve identifiers through SIMBAD, retaining the queried identifier, source URL, returned PGC/LEDA alias and identifier-section evidence. Match exact PGC IDs and CF4 group IDs against the existing registry. Use no redshift or distance value for identity matching, exclusions or selection.

Prior exposure disclosure: the abstract's aggregate inference and selected methods paragraphs have been read. The methods describe recalibrating 61 older measurements and a future expanded dataset. The paragraph discussing NGC 4874 exposed its old and revised distance and aggregate calibration impact; no new target redshift was scored. Do not claim this paper is fully blind or that a newly calibrated old object is fresh.

Decision rule: confirmed existing object/group hits exclude the corresponding target from fresh evaluation. No-hit names remain unverified until the wider historical alias audit is complete. A calibration-table target is not automatically a new distant prediction target. Preserve calibration-anchor dependence. Do not fit a new rate, substitute revised distances into the current 164 rows, import the paper's Hubble estimate or adopt a redshift-derived distance. Report which data are actually available in this release versus promised for later work.
