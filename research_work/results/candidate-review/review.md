# Which mechanisms are still candidates for the complete theory?

## Why this review matters

We now have many working calculations, but none supplies the whole photon-to-companion-to-deposit-to-gravity chain. A test can pass because it correctly calculates that a candidate fails. A good capture mechanism cannot fix the wrong photon spectrum, and a good background spectrum cannot supply missing stellar energy. This review separates usable calculations from usable complete theories and gives the next work a clear purpose.

The prior turn made progress: the thermalizer calculation was committed and independently reproduced. This review uses that released state and the current task and observable registers. It does not mark the research complete, change the fictional universe contract, or claim that an untested mechanism is impossible.

## The central gap

No currently tested candidate has simultaneously established:

1. A nonzero, adequately normalized partial energy-transfer process that preserves the required spectral and image properties.
2. Photon emission, travel, arrival timing and detection using the same matter clocks and rods.
3. A physically identified companion and finite, supported storage state.
4. Sufficient photon-funded energy and a derived joint gravitational effect on motion and lensing.
5. A compatible nonexpanding source/background history and independent observational validation.

These are scientific gaps, not missing software permissions or the five absent historical paper-build files. They do not prevent further calculations, but they do prevent calling the whole theory established. Energy conservation sets the transfer accounting; it does not supply the interaction rate, the storage mechanism or the gravitational field equation.

## Next executable priorities

### First: test an interaction that changes an assumption of the existing failures

The next microscopic comparison should specify a joint spatial and temporal material/field response, or a different photon coupling. Before fitting a desired redshift, record its fields, action, degrees of freedom, finite parameter set, state of the environment, validity range and energy destinations. Derive the energy-direction kernel, reverse transitions and source depletion from that same specification.

Why: the current point-target filter family cannot become achromatic by retuning only its frequency response. The spatial-only improvement does not fix the energy jumps. Combining their attractive features by hand could conceal contradictory dynamics.

Outcome: either an explicit kernel that escapes those particular bounds, with its costs retained, or a scoped rejection of the tested extension. Do not require observational success as the only acceptable outcome, and do not declare the complete family impossible from one failed example.

### Second: require an end-to-end light prediction before another galaxy normalization fit

For any retained kernel, predict line shifts and widths, directional diffusion, photon survival, event duration, brightness and detector clock comparisons with one parameter set. Compare against the existing conditional drift benchmark, without refitting galaxy distances. Distinguish conservation of carrier phase from stretching the separation of independent emission events.

Why: matching redshift versus distance alone can coexist with wrong time dilation, brightness or image sharpness. A fit to the final galaxy gravity profile cannot repair these earlier failures.

Outcome: a falsifiable propagation specification or a documented failure. Freeze a validation protocol before acquiring a fresh sample; all currently reused samples remain exploratory.

### Third: join the surviving source to the receiver and gravitational field

Carry the emitted companion spectrum and momentum distribution into an identified receiver. Include finite capacity, leakage, heat, radiation, orbital changes and a finite source history. Derive both motion and light deflection from the same stress source or explicit modified-gravity action. Report supply shortages conditional on the actual response, rather than assuming an arbitrary gravity multiplier.

Why: favorable source, capture and gravity formulas from different models do not automatically describe the same universe. Recycling energy also cannot permanently store that same energy elsewhere.

Outcome: a source-funded dynamical prediction or an explicit quantitative obstruction. The existing background and capture calculations remain constraints on this joined model, rather than substitutes for it.
