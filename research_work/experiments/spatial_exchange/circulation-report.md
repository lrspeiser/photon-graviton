The signed circulation is a loop integral of the vector shift in the source
equatorial plane. It is not itself an inward force, scalar mass or lensing
multiplier. Orbital wave angular momentum and vector-field spin contributions
are separately archived; their sum with matter matches the existing ledger.

The wave energy/source enhancement measured earlier is mainly a scalar-sector
effect in these examples. It has not transferred appreciable angular momentum
into X/Y or amplified the vector circulation. Even the tiny changes above are
not established as converged predictions. The short endpoint cannot demonstrate
persistence, outward propagation of a stable vortex or a physical galaxy scale.

The next substantive mechanism must address production and transport of angular
momentum, not merely increase the scalar response coefficient. A rotational
emitter or reciprocal flow-dependent coupling would require an explicit source
angular-momentum budget and complete field reactions before it can be tested.
This is a research direction, not a claimed successful replacement.
