## What was demonstrated

Initially empty X/Y channels are generated by finite internal matter excitation
and reciprocal coupling, with all source reactions derived from one Hamiltonian.
No emission or no excitation produces no X/Y field. Removing conversion produces
no Y channel. Source positions and channel energies survive the declared time,
space and rotation comparisons. This is a source-funded short-lived scalar-wave
model, not a derivation of electromagnetic photons or physical gravitons.

## New source diagnostic exposes a remaining accuracy issue

SE-1S/SE-T differentiate the wave Hamiltonian and find the actual scalar source.
For chi=200, the coarse-grid integral is 0.0017998152; the finer-grid result is
0.0016032895, about 10.92% lower. The two channel-energy terms agree much more
closely, so passing the original energy gate does not establish source accuracy.
The source combines positive kinetic and signed interaction terms and can be
more sensitive to their cancellation. A source-specific convergence test is
required before interpreting its magnitude. Do not revise the original gates
or hide their limited coverage.

SE-2 tests a changed emitter. Its mixed-emitter chi=200 example increases the
