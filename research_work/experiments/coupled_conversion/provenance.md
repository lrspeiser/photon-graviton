## Primary sources checked
[1] Qu, Jia, Edwards and Fisch, *Theory of electromagnetic wave frequency
upconversion in dynamic media*, Physical Review E98,023202 (2018).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07358
The abstract and paper record describe evolving frequency, amplitude and energy
in time-dependent media. This is related mathematical context, not proof of
our fictional constitutive law.

[2] Moussa et al., *Observation of Temporal Reflections and Broadband Frequency
Translations at Photonic Time-Interfaces* (2022 preprint; publication linked
from author record). https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07236
Laboratory switched-media frequency translation is prior work. A powered
laboratory temporal interface is not our autonomous photon-funded mechanism.

[3] Bekenstein, *Fine-structure constant: Is it really a constant?*,
Physical Review D25,1527 (1982).
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.25.1527
Also *Fine-structure constant variability, equivalence principle, and cosmology*,
D66,123514 (2002):
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.66.123514
Scalar-dependent electromagnetic and matter couplings have long-established
precedents. The two papers do not support the same simplified conclusion
about equivalence tests; no broad historical exclusion is imported here.
The present matched-medium family changes epsilon and mu together, whereas
a simple covariant Z(phi)F^2 coefficient is structurally different.

[4] Gertsenshtein, *Wave Resonance of Light and Gravitational Waves*,
Soviet Physics JETP14,84 (1962; original Russian1961).
https://jetp.ras.ru/cgi-bin/dn/e_014_01_0084.pdf
An external magnetic/electric field participates in known light/gravity-wave
conversion. It is not a derived constant fractional redshift of surviving
photons and is not a scalar-wave identity.

[5] Palessandro and Rothman, *A Simple Derivation of the Gertsenshtein Effect*
(2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02072
Further context on established photon/graviton mixing. No cosmological
background or conversion formula from this paper is used in the tests.

copied. No absence from this search establishes uniqueness.

## Public language rules for this campaign
Use "we implemented/tested this specified candidate" and "conditional result".
Do not use "our discovery of photon–graviton conversion", "never considered",
"first theory", or "derived gravity" for these calculations. New combinations
of familiar equations are not automatically novel physics. Literature overlap
must remain visible even if a numerical test succeeds.
