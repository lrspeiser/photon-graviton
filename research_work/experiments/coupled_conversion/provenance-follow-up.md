# Attribution for the unusual-law experiments
The extra sign, power and constant tests were requested by the project owner
after the baseline failures. They are explicitly labeled exploratory.
Changing a sign, exponent or coefficient is not, by itself, a claim of invention.

Scalar-dependent material masses/couplings and nonlinear coupling functions
have established antecedents. Damour and Esposito-Farese, *Tensor-scalar
gravity and binary-pulsar experiments* (1996), section I, equations1.1-1.3:
https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9602056
The primary PDF was opened and the action/definitions read. This is related
prior mathematics, not the same theory as our nonrelativistic material-rest
term or a derivation of our optical law. We do not import its gravity action,
binary-pulsar results or observational bounds into these tests.

Negative squared mass with a bounded self-interaction is also established
field-theory mathematics. Goldstone, *Field theories with Superconductor
solutions*, Il Nuovo Cimento19,154-164 (1961):
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02812722
Publisher record/abstract checked. This attribution concerns the familiar
instability mechanism, not a claimed equivalence of our real scalar fixture
to that paper's full field theory.

Follow-up searches on19 September2026 used the author/title and
negative-mass-squared/self-interaction terms. The IHES scanned1991/1992
tensor-multi-scalar PDF exceeded the fetch limit, and CERN's Goldstone record
returned a browser challenge. The1996 primary paper and Springer record above
provided the accessible sources. No detailed result is ascribed to unread
pages. No external code was copied.

The five exploratory families remain: sign reversal, nonlinear powers,
clock exponents, propagation/curvature constants, and counted material-rest
coupling. The equations and computations are test specifications; historical
novelty remains unestablished. See provenance.md for the original claim register.
