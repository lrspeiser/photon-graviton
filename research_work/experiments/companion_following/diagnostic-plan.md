# GF-1 post-screen diagnostic plan

Declared after the original screen and refinements, before these diagnostic
runs. These are exposed follow-ups, not a holdout and not a change to original
selection, scores or gates.

1. Run the common central potential alone, following coefficient zero:
   the original 12-packet chain and 16-packet ring, the ring through 256 at
   dt=0.02, and the 24-packet chain through 32 and through 64. This tests
   whether a following pass actually requires the added velocity law.
2. Extend all 12 already selected phenomenological 24-packet chains to time
   64 at dt=0.04. The original time-32 failures remain failures. Preserve
   the 20-time-unit lag cap in the original score; separately report
   threshold arrivals and a descriptive larger-lag correlation. A late
   response is not proof of finite spatial propagation.
3. Repeat the three selected conservative rings through 256 at dt=0.01.
   Compare the first exit from the declared behavioral bounds, final radius,
   and conserved quantities with dt=0.02. Agreement criteria stay 5 percent
   of max(1, metric). Long-time trajectory divergence does not by itself
   overturn a reproduced instability onset; report both.
4. Keep every result and fail flag, including a baseline that works as well
   as or better than a more complicated law.

The first run showed 31/600 phenomenological and 4/18 conservative joint
short-fixture passes. All 12 selected long chains failed at time32; all three
selected conservative rings failed by time256 despite conserved quantities.
This is why these diagnostics are warranted. No law is called a solved
gravitational interaction and no observed lensing is fitted here.
