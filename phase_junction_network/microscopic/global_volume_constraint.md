# Fixed Global Volume Constraint

**Status:** linear homogeneous conformal instability removed by a second-class pair  
**Date:** 2026-09-20

## 1. Problem exposed by the real-space reduction

The nonzero-momentum sector of the linear frame theory is positive after the four local constraints are imposed. The unrestricted periodic zero mode is different.

For the six homogeneous frame coordinates \(h_I^{(0)}\) and six conjugate momenta \(p_I^{(0)}\), the potential vanishes and the kinetic matrix is

\[
T=I-\frac12tt^T,
\]

where \(t_I\) extracts the spatial trace. Its eigenvalues are

\[
\left\{-\frac12,1,1,1,1,1\right\}.
\]

Thus the twelve-dimensional global sector has Hamiltonian inertia

\[
(1\text{ negative},6\text{ zero},5\text{ positive}).
\]

The negative direction is the homogeneous trace momentum.

## 2. Fixed-volume condition

Impose the global volume condition

\[
V=t_Ih_I^{(0)}=0.
\]

This condition is not first-class by itself. Its Hamiltonian time derivative is

\[
\dot V
=\{V,H\}
=t^TTp^{(0)}
=-\frac12t_Ip_I^{(0)}.
\]

Preservation therefore generates the secondary constraint

\[
P_V=t_Ip_I^{(0)}=0.
\]

Their Poisson bracket is

\[
\{V,P_V\}=t^Tt=3.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{V=0,\quad P_V=0}
\]

form a second-class pair.

## 3. Reduced global sector

The pair removes one coordinate and one momentum, reducing the global phase space from 12 to 10 dimensions.

The reduced Hamiltonian inertia is

\[
\boxed{(0\text{ negative},5\text{ zero},5\text{ positive}).}
\]

The negative homogeneous conformal pair is gone. What remains is:

- five trace-free homogeneous shape coordinates with zero potential;
- five positive trace-free homogeneous momenta.

These are global shear/shape moduli, not propagating finite-momentum modes.

## 5. Interpretation

A fixed-volume or unimodular-type rule is a mathematically consistent way to remove the periodic homogeneous conformal instability at the linear level. It does not alter the positive nonzero-momentum tensor spectrum.

This provides a concrete answer to the global instability discovered in the full real-space check:

> the negative mode is removed by fixing total volume and enforcing the trace-momentum condition generated by time preservation.

## 6. Limitations

This is not yet a full solution to vacuum energy or cosmology.

It does not establish:

- why the microscopic Phase Junction network enforces fixed total volume;
- whether global volume should be exactly fixed or dynamically sequestered;
- how local vacuum energy enters the nonlinear scalar constraint;
- whether the remaining five homogeneous shear moduli should be fixed by boundary conditions or lifted dynamically;
- closure of the corresponding nonlinear constraint algebra;
- the observed large-scale cosmological behavior.

The next nonlinear model should compare at least three options:

1. exact fixed four-volume or spatial volume;
2. a global conjugate sequestering variable;
3. a dynamical volume mode with a bounded positive Hamiltonian.

For the current linear periodic theory, however, the fixed-volume second-class pair removes exactly the one negative global canonical direction and no positive propagating tensor mode.
