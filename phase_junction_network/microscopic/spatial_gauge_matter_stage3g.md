## 2. Finite patch

Use a periodic \(2\times2\) spatial torus with:

- four sites;
- eight positive-oriented spin-1 links;
- four plaquettes;
- one dynamical charge \(q=+1\);
- one dynamical charge \(q=-1\).

The total charge is zero, so the periodic Gauss law has an exact physical sector.

The pure-gauge physical Hilbert space has

\[
115
\]

\[

The basis is constructed by exact Gauss-law enumeration rather than by adding a penalty and later projecting.

## 8. Nonzero-momentum Ward identity

At

\[

\]

\[
\rho_k=\sum_re^{-ik\cdot r}\rho_r

and the finite-difference current divergence

\[
(1-e^{-ik_\mu})j_\mu(k).
\]

The operator Ward relation

\[
\boxed{
i[H,\rho_k]
+\sum_\mu(1-e^{-ik_\mu})j_\mu(k)
=0
}
\]

\[
8.28\times10^{-18}.
\]

For the computed low-energy eigenstates,

\[

=0
\]

with maximum residual

\[
2.02\times10^{-15}.
\]

This is the first nonzero-momentum finite Ward result in the spatial photon phase.
